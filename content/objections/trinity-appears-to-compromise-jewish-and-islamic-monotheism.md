# Objection

From Jewish and Islamic perspectives, Christianity appears to introduce plurality precisely where revelation requires uncompromising divine unity.

The objection is not answered by saying merely that Christians also use the word **one**.

A Christian model must explain how:

- Father, Son, and Spirit are genuinely distinct;
- each can be spoken of in divine terms;
- and yet there are not three gods.

Only after coherence is addressed does the separate question of positive evidence arise.
