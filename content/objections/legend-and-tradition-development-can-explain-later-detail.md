# Objection

"Early belief" and "every later narrative detail is early" are different propositions.

A skeptic can grant that resurrection proclamation began very early while arguing that later tomb and appearance stories became more detailed through ordinary transmission.

## Constraint

This cannot explain away the earliest Pauline material, because that material itself requires an origin account.
