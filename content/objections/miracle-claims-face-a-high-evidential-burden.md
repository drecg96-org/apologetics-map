# Objection

The skeptical point is not simply that "miracles cannot happen."

If God exists, miracles may be metaphysically possible. The dispute is whether the available evidence warrants believing that a particular extraordinary event actually occurred and had a supernatural cause.

## The evidential question

A miracle claim must be compared with alternatives such as:

- mistake or memory distortion,
- legendary development,
- misinterpretation of an unusual but natural event,
- incomplete evidence,
- deception,
- or some other natural explanation.

The relevant standard is not a magic slogan like "extraordinary claims require extraordinary evidence." It is whether the total evidence makes the miracle hypothesis sufficiently credible relative to its competitors and background knowledge.

For Christianity, this issue becomes especially important at the resurrection.
