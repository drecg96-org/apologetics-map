# Objection

Even granting diversity in Jewish messianic expectation, Christianity faces a concrete Jewish objection: the world after Jesus' ministry did not look like the completed messianic age.

Israel was not gathered under a Davidic ruler, universal peace did not arrive, and the Temple was not restored by Jesus.

The later Christian appeal to a future return can answer this only if a **two-stage fulfillment** is independently warranted. Otherwise the move can look like redefining failed expectations after the fact.
