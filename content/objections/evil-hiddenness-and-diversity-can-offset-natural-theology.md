# Objection

Positive natural theology cannot be evaluated in a vacuum.

If personal theism makes some features of reality more expected, the same hypothesis must answer features that critics regard as less expected on a perfectly good personal God.

The total-evidence question is therefore broader than whether any individual pro-theistic argument is sound.
