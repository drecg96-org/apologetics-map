# Objection

Punishment is normally thought to track the guilt of the person punished.

So a crude substitution model creates a moral problem:

> Why would justice be served by deliberately punishing someone who did not commit the offense?

A response must explain representation, union, voluntary self-giving, or another mechanism rather than merely repeat that "someone had to pay."
