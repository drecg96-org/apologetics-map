# Objection

Observer selection matters.

If a sufficiently varied multiverse exists, no observer should be surprised to occupy a region compatible with observers.

## Remaining dispute

A multiverse is not a free explanatory token. Its physical motivation, probability measure, and whether its generating structure itself raises fine-tuning questions all remain contested.
