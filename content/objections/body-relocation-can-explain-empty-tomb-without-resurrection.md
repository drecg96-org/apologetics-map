# Objection

An empty tomb, by itself, does not entail resurrection.

If the body was moved by authorities, associates, grave workers, or someone else, the tomb could later be empty for entirely natural reasons.

## Scope limit

Relocation explains **an empty tomb**. It does not by itself explain sincere appearance experiences or the rise of resurrection belief.

A full naturalistic model would therefore need additional mechanisms.
