# Objection

Fine-tuning rhetoric often uses very small numbers as though there were an agreed random generator over possible universes.

But probabilities require a measure.

Without a defensible distribution over constants, laws, or initial conditions, claims of extreme improbability can outrun what the formal model warrants.
