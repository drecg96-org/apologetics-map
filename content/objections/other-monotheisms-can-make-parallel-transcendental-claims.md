# Objection

A critic can grant much of the transcendental setup and still resist the Christian conclusion.

Judaism and Islam also affirm, in importantly different ways, a transcendent Creator, divine knowledge, providence, revelation, moral authority, and an ordered creation. A Muslim or Jewish thinker can therefore ask why those resources could not ground the same logical and epistemic features that the Christian says require God.

John Johnson makes the Islamic version explicit: if a Christian apologist can begin from Christian revelation and argue that only that worldview makes intelligibility possible, a Muslim apologist may attempt the same strategy with Islamic revelation.

## The symmetry problem

The problem is not solved merely by saying "the Christian worldview is self-attesting" or "the rival worldview is internally impossible." Those are conclusions that need argument.

A successful Christian-specific TAG must identify a relevant feature of the rival worldview, show why it genuinely undermines the preconditions of rationality, and show why Christianity avoids the same problem. Otherwise both sides can simply assert that only their revelation grounds reason.
