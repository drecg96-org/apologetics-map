# Objection

The response cannot simply say:

> God might have unknown reasons for anything.

If that move were unconstrained, it could undermine confidence in ordinary moral reasoning and also weaken positive theistic arguments that infer divine intentions from the world.

A viable skeptical theism needs principled limits.
