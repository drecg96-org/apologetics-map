# Objection

The demand for an ultimate explanation may simply be too strong.

A skeptic can say:

> The cosmos exists contingently, and there is no deeper explanation.

That answer may feel unsatisfying without being incoherent.

The argument therefore needs a reason to prefer a necessary ground over brute contingent reality, not merely a preference for explanation.
