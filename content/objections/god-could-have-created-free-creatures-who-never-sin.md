# Objection

The skeptic can grant, for the sake of argument, that freedom is valuable and still ask:

> Why must genuine freedom include an actual Fall?

There is no obvious contradiction in saying of a person:

- the person could choose wrongly,
- no external force determines the choice,
- and in fact the person freely chooses rightly every time.

If such a person is possible, why could an omniscient and omnipotent God not create a world populated by people like that?

This is stronger than the claim that God should make robots. It asks for **free agents who happen always to choose the good**.

Any free-will defense therefore needs an account of why that world may not be feasible for God, or why a different kind of freedom or providence is at stake.
