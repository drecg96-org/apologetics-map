# Objection

The companions-in-guilt argument gains force only if the opponent is unwilling to give up objective epistemic normativity.

A global normative error theorist can accept the wider consequence: ordinary judgments about what one morally, practically, or epistemically **ought** to do or believe systematically fail to report stance-independent normative facts.

## Cost of the view

This is highly revisionary. It must reconstruct rational criticism, evidence-responsiveness, and practical deliberation without the objective normative facts many people think those practices presuppose.

But accepting a large cost is not the same thing as holding a contradiction.

## Debate consequence

Once the opponent explicitly accepts global normative anti-realism, the epistemic companions-in-guilt line has reached its natural endpoint.
