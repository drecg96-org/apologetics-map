# Objection

A Christian appeal to "fulfillment" can become a verbal shield if fulfillment means the original promises no longer constrain the interpretation.

The Jewish objection asks whether God can be said to keep covenant while:

- Israel's covenantal identity is transferred to another people;
- Torah is treated as obsolete in a way its own texts do not anticipate;
- concrete restoration promises are spiritualized;
- and continuing Jewish covenant life is treated only as error.

A Christian model should therefore state exactly what continues and what changes.
