# Objection

A conflict between the Qur'an and today's Bible need not be a conflict between the Qur'an and the revelation originally given to Moses or Jesus.

The Muslim defender can distinguish the original **Tawrat** and **Injil** from later textual forms and argue that transmission, editing, loss, or deliberate alteration explains the mismatch.

## What this move requires

The corruption hypothesis becomes historically substantive only when it specifies:

- what was altered;
- approximately when it was altered;
- whether the alteration concerns wording or interpretation;
- what pre-Islamic manuscript and citation evidence should look like if the hypothesis is true.

Without those details, "corruption" risks becoming an unfalsifiable repair whenever the sources disagree.
