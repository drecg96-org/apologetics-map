# Objection

A list of many arguments does not automatically produce a strong cumulative case.

If several claims depend on the same late tradition, or if a prediction is recorded only after the event, the pieces are not independent in the way the argument requires.

The evaluation therefore has to ask, claim by claim:

- what is the earliest source?
- how securely is it dated?
- is the wording specific enough to discriminate prophecy from hindsight?
- is the source independent of the claim it is being used to prove?
- what rival explanation fits the same data?

This objection does not assume Muhammad was not a prophet. It specifies the historical work needed to establish the case.
