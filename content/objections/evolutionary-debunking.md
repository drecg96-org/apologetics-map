# Objection

Evolution strongly influenced human evaluative tendencies. Natural selection rewards reproductive success, not philosophical accuracy about independent moral facts.

Sharon Street's influential challenge asks how a realist can explain the relation between those evolutionary influences and the independent moral truths the realist posits.

If there is no truth-tracking connection, widespread moral reliability can look coincidental. If there is a connection, the realist must explain it without simply assuming the moral facts.

## Scope

This is primarily an epistemic challenge to our access to moral truth, though it can indirectly pressure moral realism itself.
