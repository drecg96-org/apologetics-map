# Objection to the skeptical inference

Showing that natural mechanisms **exist** is not the same as showing that they jointly explain the specific historical pattern.

The skeptical model must still account for the shape of early resurrection belief, the named appearance traditions, and any empty-tomb evidence the reader grants.

This objection targets the comparative inference rather than denying that bereavement experiences or tradition development occur.
