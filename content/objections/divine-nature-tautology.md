# Objection

Suppose goodness is grounded in God's nature. A critic can then ask whether the statement 'God is good' has substantive content or reduces to something like 'God has God's nature.'

If the theory cannot independently characterize the relevant excellences, the proposed solution may look circular.

This is a stronger follow-up to Euthyphro than simply repeating the original dilemma, because it directly tests whether the third-option response explains anything.
