# Objection

The Christian case sometimes criticizes alternatives for not explaining every datum with one mechanism.

But ordinary history is frequently multi-causal.

A skeptic can ask whether a package such as:

- genuine visionary experience;
- social reinforcement;
- tradition development;
- and uncertainty or relocation regarding the tomb

has higher background plausibility than a miracle even if it is less unified.

## Cost

Mixed explanations can become ad hoc if each new datum is met with a new unsupported mechanism. Their complexity must count in the comparison.
