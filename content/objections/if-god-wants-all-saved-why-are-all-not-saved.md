# Objection

The tension can be stated without assuming that people earn heaven:

1. God desires all people to be saved.
2. God is omnipotent.
3. A perfectly loving God would not abandon a person unnecessarily.
4. Yet many Christian traditions hold that some people are finally lost.

Why do (1) and (2) not produce universal salvation?

A free-will answer says God will not coerce love or repentance. But that opens another question: **must successful divine persuasion count as coercion?**

If God has unlimited time, perfect knowledge of every person, and unlimited resources for truthful revelation and grace, a universalist can argue that God could eventually win every person freely.

So "God respects freedom" is a substantive answer, not an automatic conversation-stopper.
