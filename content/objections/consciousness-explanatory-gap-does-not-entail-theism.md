# Objection

A live explanatory problem is not automatically positive evidence for a specific alternative.

Physicalist theories may improve, and some versions do not promise the kind of reduction the objection assumes.

A successful consciousness argument therefore needs comparative predictive or explanatory advantage, not merely current mystery.
