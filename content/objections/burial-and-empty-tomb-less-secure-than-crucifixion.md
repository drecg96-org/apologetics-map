# Objection

A resurrection argument should not call every disputed premise a "minimal fact."

Jesus' crucifixion is historically much firmer than:

- the exact burial circumstances;
- Joseph of Arimathea's role;
- identification of a particular tomb;
- or the historicity of its later discovery as empty.

A skeptic can therefore proceed through the branch while withholding the empty tomb rather than being forced to reject the whole discussion.
