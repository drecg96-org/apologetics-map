# Objection

A secular moral theory need not reduce morality to preference. Some realists argue that moral truths are grounded in natural facts about flourishing, suffering, rational agency, human nature, or reasons for action.

If such an account can explain both value and genuine obligation without appealing to God, premise 2 of the moral argument loses force.

## The crux

The debate is not whether flourishing matters. The harder question is whether descriptive facts about flourishing are sufficient to generate genuinely normative conclusions about what anyone *ought* to do, including an agent who rejects the relevant goals.
