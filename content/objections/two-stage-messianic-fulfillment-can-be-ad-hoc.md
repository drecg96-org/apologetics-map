# Objection

A theory can always preserve a failed prediction by moving the unmet part into the future.

So the Christian two-stage answer has evidential force only if there are reasons **independent of the failed expectations themselves** to believe:

- Jesus was vindicated after death;
- early Christians did not simply abandon the concrete restoration hope;
- the relevant Jewish and Christian texts allow an inaugurated-but-incomplete pattern;
- and the second stage is not merely an unfalsifiable postponement.

This leaves a genuine Jewish-Christian crux rather than declaring either side victorious by definition.
