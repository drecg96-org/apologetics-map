# Objection

A natural explanation need not accuse the earliest witnesses of lying.

Bereavement and religious contexts can produce vivid experiences of a deceased person. That supplies a natural mechanism for at least some sincere appearance claims.

## Limits

The model becomes harder if it must explain:

- independently attested group encounters;
- strongly physical interaction details;
- the empty tomb, if granted;
- or why the experiences generated specifically bodily-resurrection belief.

Those are separate evidential questions rather than automatic defeaters.
