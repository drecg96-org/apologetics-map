# Objection

Followers whose messianic expectations were shattered by crucifixion might have had strong psychological and social incentives to reinterpret the failure rather than abandon the movement.

Cognitive-dissonance theory supplies one possible mechanism.

## Important caution

This should **not** be treated as a complete resurrection theory or as a proven parallel.

The famous modern failed-prophecy case used to motivate this analogy has itself been challenged by recent archival work. Application to Jesus' followers is therefore indirect and contestable.

At most, dissonance may be one component of a broader model.
