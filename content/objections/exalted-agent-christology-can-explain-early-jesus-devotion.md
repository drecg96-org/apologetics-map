# Objection

Ancient Jewish and Christian categories included exalted angels, agents, messianic figures, Wisdom traditions, and heavenly mediators.

Therefore an early, extremely high view of Jesus does not mechanically yield later Nicene metaphysics.

A rival explanation is that resurrection faith elevated Jesus to God's supreme agent while preserving an ontological distinction between Jesus and the one God.

The debate turns on which devotional practices and scriptural patterns are compatible with merely created agency.
