# Objection

People and cultures disagree deeply about moral questions. Some critics argue that the extent and pattern of disagreement are easier to explain if moral judgments reflect attitudes, interests, traditions, or social practices rather than a shared objective moral reality.

## Strong form

The objection is not merely that disagreement exists. People also disagree about scientific facts. The stronger claim is that moral disagreement may be unusually persistent, resistant to neutral methods of resolution, and correlated with social formation.

If that explanatory story is right, moral realism loses some of its initial plausibility.
