# Objection

Consent can make sacrificial suffering morally different from involuntary victimization.

But if the theory specifically says **punishment** is transferred, the retributive question remains: why does punishing a non-guilty bearer satisfy the principle that punishment should track guilt?

Penal models need more than voluntariness.
