# Objection

Religious experiences do not arrive with an interpretation-neutral label.

Different people understand superficially similar experiences through incompatible religious frameworks, while some experiences have plausible psychological explanations.

That does not make all experience worthless, but it can sharply limit tradition-specific or metaphysical conclusions.
