# Objection

Mistaken identity is possible in principle, especially for ambiguous or distant encounters.

## Why it is a weak global theory

It does not naturally explain:

- Paul's experience;
- the origin of a broad resurrection proclamation;
- repeated traditions attributed to known associates;
- or an empty tomb, if one grants it.

The map includes this node because M2 compares the real alternative space, not because every alternative deserves equal weight.
