# Objection

"Jesus was worshiped early" and "the Nicene doctrine of the Trinity is true" are not the same claim.

The historical data can establish an early puzzle inside Jewish monotheism while leaving open multiple explanations:

- Jesus was understood as uniquely exalted but subordinate;
- Jesus participated in divine identity in some strong sense;
- different early Christian groups held different models;
- later creedal theology systematized tensions that earlier texts did not state in later metaphysical language.

M5 therefore treats **earliness**, **meaning**, **coherence**, and **truth** as separate questions.
