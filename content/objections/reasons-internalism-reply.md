# Objection

The realist argument assumes that a person's lack of desire does not remove the person's moral reason.

A reasons internalist can dispute that assumption. On influential internalist views, a normative reason must connect in the right way to the agent's motivational set, perhaps after correcting factual error and improving deliberation.

That means the totally indifferent agent in the thought experiment may simply lack the categorical reason the realist says exists.

## What follows

This is not a contradiction. It is a substantive theory of practical reasons with a clear cost: sufficiently different agents may lack reasons that ordinary moral discourse often treats as universal.
