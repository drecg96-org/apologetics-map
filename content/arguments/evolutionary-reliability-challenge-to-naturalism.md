# Argument

The formal conclusion is deliberately negative:

> evolutionary naturalism faces a reliability challenge.

It does not conclude:

> therefore Christianity.

For M3 the value of the argument is comparative: whether theism better fits our unavoidable practice of trusting reason when combined with other lines.
