# Argument

Suppose we mean something stronger than "we now prefer this."

If an earlier society could be morally mistaken even while its members approved of a practice, and if a later reform is better because both societies are answerable to a standard beyond their own approval, then current social acceptance is not what makes the moral judgment true.

That is enough to move beyond simple society-relative morality.

## Limit

This does not identify the correct moral theory, prove every moral judgment objective, or establish theism. It supports only the narrower claim that at least some moral evaluation is not constituted by prevailing social approval.
