# Argument

This is not the rule "earlier always wins."

Later evidence can overturn earlier evidence.

The narrower principle is that **a contradiction needs a reason to prefer the correcting source**. In the Islam-Christianity case, that pushes the debate toward the independent warrant for the Qur'an and Muhammad rather than allowing either side simply to quote its own scripture.
