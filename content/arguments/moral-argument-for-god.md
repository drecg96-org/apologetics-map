# Argument

A common contemporary form can be stated roughly as:

1. Objective moral values and duties exist.
2. A necessarily good personal foundation explains objective moral value and obligation better than the leading alternatives.
3. Therefore, objective morality provides evidence for such a personal moral foundation.

Some versions are framed deductively, but the comparative version is more transparent about where the real dispute lies: premise 2.

## What the argument is not

It is not the claim that atheists cannot be good, cannot know right from wrong, or secretly believe in God. Those claims confuse grounding with knowledge or behavior.

## Main pressure points

The strongest objections are not simple relativism. They include robust secular moral realism, naturalistic accounts of value, error theory, evolutionary debunking arguments, and the Euthyphro dilemma.

A strong presentation should name those rivals directly rather than pretending the only alternatives are God or moral chaos.
