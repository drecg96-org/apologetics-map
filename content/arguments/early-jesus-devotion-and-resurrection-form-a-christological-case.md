# Argument

The case is cumulative rather than a one-verse proof.

Very early Christians who remained embedded in Jewish monotheistic categories nevertheless gave Jesus an extraordinary devotional and theological role. If the resurrection is additionally interpreted as an act of divine vindication, the combination creates pressure toward a very high Christology.

The question remains whether **divine identity** is the best explanation of that data.
