# Argument

This map gives the skeptical alternative the same formal treatment as the Christian case.

It does **not** say:

> The disciples made it up.

Instead it proposes that sincere experiences can occur naturally and that early convictions can later be expressed in increasingly detailed narratives.

## What the argument establishes if successful

Only that a supernatural resurrection is **not required** by the surviving historical data.

It does not prove that Jesus remained dead in every metaphysical sense.

## Main pressure points

The model is weaker if:

- the empty tomb is historically strong;
- group or physical appearance traditions resist individual-vision explanations;
- early embodied-resurrection language is hard to derive from the proposed experiences;
- or the multi-cause package becomes more ad hoc than the resurrection hypothesis.

Those are exactly the places where the opposing case should press.
