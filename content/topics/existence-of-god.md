# Existence of God

This topic is intentionally not written as "arguments for God."

It is the shared philosophical space in which theism, atheism, agnosticism, and local skepticism can be represented on their own terms. The debate should begin by identifying what the other person is actually claiming and what evidence or argument is doing the work for them.

## Skeptical diagnostic

The opening diagnostic separates:

- lack of belief or suspended judgment,
- the positive judgment that God probably does not exist,
- and skepticism aimed specifically at Christianity rather than at every possible God.

Only after that distinction should the map ask which considerations matter most: evil, hiddenness, religious diversity, the success or failure of natural-theology arguments, miracle evidence, or a more Christianity-specific historical or moral objection.
