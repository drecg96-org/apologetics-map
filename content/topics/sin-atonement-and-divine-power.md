# Sin, Atonement, and Divine Power

This branch separates several questions that are often bundled together:

1. **If God is omnipotent, why was the cross needed at all?**
2. **Could God simply forgive everyone without sacrifice?**
3. **If God wants people saved, why is salvation not automatic?**
4. **Why create humans with the possibility of falling into sin?**
5. **Does meaningful freedom require God to give up some of his power?**
6. **Even if freedom explains some moral evil, what about natural suffering and apparently gratuitous evil?**

The map should not answer all six with a single slogan.

Christianity broadly agrees that humanity is alienated from God by sin and that Christ's death and resurrection are central to reconciliation. Christians disagree, however, about the exact theory of atonement, the relation between divine sovereignty and human freedom, and whether every person will ultimately be reconciled.

Likewise, a free-will defense can address a **logical** incompatibility claim without fully answering the **evidential** problem raised by the amount and distribution of suffering in the actual world.
