# Question

A skeptical theist does not need to claim that a specific tragedy has an obvious purpose.

The narrower reply is epistemic:

> Given the enormous gap between finite human knowledge and the knowledge attributed to God, how confident should we be that our inability to identify a justifying reason is evidence that none exists?

That reply has force only if it can avoid collapsing ordinary moral and inductive reasoning.
