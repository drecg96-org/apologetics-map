# Question

Two dates matter:

1. **When was the surviving text written?**
2. **How old is the tradition embedded in that text?**

First Corinthians is earlier than the canonical Gospels, and Paul explicitly says that he received and delivered resurrection tradition.

That makes a purely late-legend explanation difficult. It does **not** make the early interpretation infallible.

The next question is semantic as well as chronological: what did these early Christians mean by saying Jesus was "raised"?
