# Question

Fine-tuning is best framed as comparative evidence rather than as a probability slogan.

The relevant questions are:

- What exactly is fine-tuned?
- Relative to which probability measure?
- Is deeper physical necessity possible?
- How much work can observer selection or a multiverse do?
- What should we predict if there is a purposive designer?

Only after those questions does the inference to design become meaningful.
