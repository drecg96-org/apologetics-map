# Question

Cumulative reasoning matters only if the lines add something beyond repetition.

M3 therefore asks two questions simultaneously:

1. Do the conclusions converge on a coherent theistic profile?
2. How independent are the evidential bases and assumptions?

The map provides both an affirmative cumulative argument and a skeptical counter-argument.
