# Question

Christianity identifies Jesus as Israel's Messiah. Mainstream Judaism does not.

A fair comparison has to ask at least two things separately:

- **What did Jewish messianic expectation actually include?**
- **Does the Christian claim that some messianic promises are inaugurated now and completed later adequately answer the Jewish objection that the expected age of peace, restoration, and redemption did not arrive with Jesus?**

## Questions to develop next

Future branches should distinguish biblical texts from later rabbinic formulations, because Jewish messianic expectation was not a single uniform checklist in the first century. They should also examine Davidic kingship, restoration of Israel, the Temple, peace among the nations, suffering and exaltation motifs, Danielic "Son of Man" material, and whether a two-stage messianic fulfillment is exegetically warranted or ad hoc.

This node is a comparison point, not yet an argument that Jesus either did or did not satisfy the relevant expectations.
