# Question

Before asking whether God raised Jesus, separate ordinary historical claims from miracle claims.

The useful starting point is not:

> Do you accept the whole Gospel story?

It is:

> What can we responsibly say about Jesus using ordinary historical reasoning?

For the resurrection branch, the first high-confidence checkpoint is Jesus' execution by crucifixion. From there, the map can ask what happened after his death without smuggling the answer into the premises.
