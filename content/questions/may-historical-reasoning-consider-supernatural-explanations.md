# Question

At this stage, the dispute can change type.

A skeptic may grant much of the history and still say:

> Historical method can tell us what people claimed and experienced, but not that God acted.

A defender may reply:

> If supernatural action is possible and the hypothesis has superior explanatory power, excluding it by rule begs the question.

This is a real methodological disagreement, not merely a dispute about one Gospel detail.
