# Crux question

Why should fuller information, coherence, impartiality, or some rational procedure determine what a person **ought** to endorse?

There are at least two broad answers.

- **Constructivist answer:** the authority is generated by standards internal to rational agency, deliberation, or the evaluative standpoint.
- **Realist pressure:** describing some forms of deliberation as genuinely better or more rational may already rely on normative standards that are not created by the agent's current attitudes.

The dispute is therefore no longer about whether actual preferences can be corrected. Both sides can allow that. It is about what makes the correcting standard authoritative.
