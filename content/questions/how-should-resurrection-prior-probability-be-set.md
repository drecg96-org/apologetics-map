# Question

Two people can inspect the same historical record and still differ because they begin with different justified background models.

Questions include:

- How strongly does ordinary experience count against resurrection?
- Is God's existence independently plausible?
- If God exists, is intervention of this sort antecedently plausible?
- How reliable are the relevant reports?
- How much explanatory advantage would be needed to overcome a low prior?

Making the prior explicit is better than hiding it behind slogans on either side.
