# Diagnostic question

The strongest skeptical case is rarely just one sentence such as "prove God."

Ask which consideration carries the most weight:

- **Evil and suffering:** the amount or distribution of apparently pointless suffering seems more expected without a perfectly good God.
- **Divine hiddenness:** sincere people can remain open to God yet unconvinced that God exists.
- **Religious diversity:** informed, sincere people reach incompatible religious conclusions.
- **Natural theology does not compel:** arguments for God may depend on premises or explanatory judgments a reasonable skeptic can reject.
- **Miracles and resurrection:** extraordinary historical claims may require a stronger evidential bridge than ordinary historical claims.

This diagnostic is not meant to force one branch. Several may reinforce one another.
