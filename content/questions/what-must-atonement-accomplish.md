# Question

"Why did Jesus have to die?" has no single answer until **have to** and **atonement** are specified.

Christian models emphasize different problems:

- guilt and judgment;
- damaged relationship;
- bondage to sin and death;
- corruption and healing;
- representation and union;
- revelation of divine love;
- moral transformation.

An objection to one model should not automatically be treated as an objection to every model.
