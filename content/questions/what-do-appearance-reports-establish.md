# Question

There are several distinct propositions hiding inside "Jesus appeared":

1. Some followers sincerely believed they experienced Jesus after his death.
2. Specific named people are associated with appearance traditions.
3. Some traditions describe appearances to groups.
4. Later Gospel narratives depict physical interaction.
5. The external cause was the objectively risen Jesus.

The first claims can have stronger historical support than the fifth.

A good explanation must ask what natural experiences can account for and where they stop.
