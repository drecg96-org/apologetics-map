# Question

Christianity's claim to fulfill Israel's story faces a sharper question than whether Christians use the Hebrew Bible.

If "new covenant" means that God's covenant with Israel was simply discarded and replaced by an unrelated Gentile church, the claim sits uneasily with both Jewish self-understanding and important New Testament texts.

The live alternatives include replacement, fulfillment, covenant expansion, differentiated callings, and other models.
