# Question

Christianity emerged from Judaism and claims continuity with Israel's Scriptures, God, and promises. Judaism rejects the claim that allegiance to Jesus is the proper completion of that covenantal story.

This branch should ask:

- What did the Hebrew Bible say would be permanent about Torah, covenant, land, Temple, priesthood, and Israel?
- What kinds of covenantal renewal or "new covenant" were anticipated within Jewish Scripture itself?
- Did Jesus and the earliest Jewish Christians understand themselves as abandoning Torah, reinterpreting it, or announcing its fulfillment?
- How should Gentile inclusion be understood?
- Which Christian claims become supersessionist, and which forms of Christian theology preserve an ongoing role for Israel?
- Are Christian readings of Israel's Scriptures plausible in their original contexts as well as retrospectively in light of Jesus?

The aim is to test continuity rather than assume that "later" automatically means "fulfilled."
