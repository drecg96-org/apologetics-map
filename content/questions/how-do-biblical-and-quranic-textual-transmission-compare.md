# Question

Claims like "the Bible was corrupted" or "the Qur'an has never changed" compress several different historical questions.

A fair comparison should ask separately:

- how early the surviving witnesses are;
- how much textual variation exists;
- whether variants can be identified and compared;
- whether a standard text was imposed or gradually stabilized;
- whether multiple authorized readings remain;
- and how confidently an earlier attainable text can be reconstructed.

Even excellent preservation would establish access to a text, not automatically that the text is revelation.
