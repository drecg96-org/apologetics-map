# Question

A cumulative case can include first-person or testimonial religious experience without making it infallible.

The key issue is whether such experience deserves some initial evidential weight and how strongly that weight is defeated by:

- religious diversity;
- psychological explanations;
- unreliable contexts;
- or incompatible doctrinal interpretations.
