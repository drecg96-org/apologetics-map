# Question

The historical claims have different evidential strength.

A careful map should distinguish at least:

- **crucifixion/death** — very strong historical footing;
- **burial** — substantial but less secure;
- **early resurrection proclamation** — strong evidence that the belief arose early;
- **appearance experiences/reports** — strong enough to demand explanation, while their cause remains disputed;
- **reported empty tomb** — clearly present in the Gospel tradition;
- **historical empty tomb** — a further and more contested inference;
- **God raised Jesus** — a causal and metaphysical conclusion, not a raw datum.

The next steps ask when the sources arise and what the earliest resurrection language meant.
