# Question

The issue is not simply whether knowledge causes an event.

Infallible foreknowledge creates a modal problem: if God already infallibly believed what you will do, in what sense are alternatives still genuinely open?

The debate includes eternity solutions, Ockhamist dependence accounts, Molinism, compatibilism, open theism, and denials of alternate-possibility requirements.
