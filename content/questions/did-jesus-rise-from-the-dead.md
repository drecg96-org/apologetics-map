# Question

The resurrection is a particularly high-leverage discriminator because Christianity treats Jesus' death and resurrection as central, while the Qur'an gives a materially different account of the crucifixion.

The historical question should be separated into smaller claims:

- Was Jesus executed by crucifixion?
- How early are the claims that he appeared alive afterward?
- What did the earliest witnesses mean by "resurrection"?
- Which facts about the origin of resurrection belief are broadly granted, and which are disputed?
- What naturalistic explanations have been proposed?
- What assumptions about miracles or divine action affect the historical inference?
- How should a seventh-century revelatory denial or reinterpretation be weighed against earlier sources about a first-century event?

This branch should compare the strongest historical explanations and then ask what follows for Christianity and Islam.
