# Question

"Resurrection" should not be left undefined.

Paul distinguishes the present body from the transformed resurrection body, but his language is still embodied rather than a simple claim that Jesus' soul survived death or that his influence lived on.

That matters because some skeptical explanations can account for **belief in continued presence** more easily than they account for an early conviction of transformed embodied life.

At the same time, embodied resurrection language does not by itself prove that a tomb was empty. That is the next separate historical question.
