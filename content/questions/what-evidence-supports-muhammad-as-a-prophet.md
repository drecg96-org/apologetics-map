# Question

If Islam is to correct earlier Jewish and Christian claims, the comparison should examine the **positive warrant** for Muhammad's prophetic authority.

Contemporary Muslim apologetic cases commonly appeal cumulatively to:

- the Qur'an and its literary or theological qualities;
- Muhammad's character and sincerity;
- the coherence and moral power of his message;
- reported miracles;
- reported fulfilled predictions;
- his accomplishments and historical impact;
- continuity with earlier monotheistic revelation.

Each line should be tested at the level of its actual evidence rather than accepted or dismissed as a package.
