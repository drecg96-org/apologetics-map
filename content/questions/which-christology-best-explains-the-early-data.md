# Question

Several important points can be true at once:

- devotion to Jesus appears very early;
- the mature Nicene formula uses later conceptual language;
- Jewish monotheism supplies both constraints and categories for interpreting Jesus;
- resurrection belief profoundly shaped early Christian claims;
- and historical data alone may underdetermine the final metaphysical account.

M5 therefore keeps the inference visible instead of silently moving from "early Christians revered Jesus" to "Nicene Christianity is proven."
