# Question

If several natural-theology arguments work, what profile emerges?

Potentially:

- necessary or non-contingent;
- transcendent of contingent physical reality;
- rational or purposive;
- fundamentally mental or personal;
- morally significant or good.

But stronger classical attributes still need argument. And none of this yet selects Christianity.
