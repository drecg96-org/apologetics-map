# Main question

Do not start by arguing against "atheism" until the position has been identified.

People use the labels *atheist*, *agnostic*, *nonbeliever*, and *skeptic* in different ways. A productive conversation should first determine which claim is actually on the table.

## Three common positions

1. **Lack of belief / suspension:** "I am not convinced that God exists." This does not necessarily assert that God does not exist.
2. **Positive atheism:** "I think God does not exist, or probably does not exist." This is a stronger claim and can be supported by positive arguments.
3. **Christianity-specific skepticism:** "I am open to God or some ultimate reality, but I do not think Christianity has established its distinctive claims."

These positions carry different argumentative burdens.

The next useful question is not "Which label are you?" but **what evidence or consideration is doing the most work in your present view?**
