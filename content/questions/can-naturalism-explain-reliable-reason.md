# Question

A reliability argument is not the claim that evolution cannot produce intelligence.

The sharper question is whether a reflective naturalist has adequate reason to trust the cognitive faculties produced by unguided evolutionary processes, especially beyond immediate fitness-relevant tasks.

The naturalist has serious replies, so the branch is mapped as a contested epistemic challenge rather than as a quick proof of God.
