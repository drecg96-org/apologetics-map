# Question

Why does contingent reality exist rather than not exist?

The dispute is not over whether every thing has a cause. It is whether an adequate explanation of contingent reality can stop with:

- an unexplained contingent fact;
- an infinite or total collection of contingent facts;
- or whether it requires something non-contingent.

The conclusion should remain "necessary ground" until further attributes are argued.
