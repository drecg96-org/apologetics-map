# Question

The map should not force a skeptic to grant the empty tomb.

The positive case appeals to early burial tradition, women in Mark's discovery narrative, the spread of empty-tomb tradition, and the fit with bodily resurrection proclamation.

The skeptical case presses burial uncertainty, literary dependence, later narrative development, and the gap between **a reported empty tomb** and **a historically established empty tomb**.

Both routes remain live. Whichever way the reader leans, appearance claims still require separate analysis.
