# Question

"Is hell fair?" is under-specified.

Different Christian models include:

- everlasting conscious punishment;
- irreversible self-exclusion;
- destruction or conditional immortality;
- temporary corrective punishment;
- universal reconciliation.

M4 evaluates the objections by model rather than presenting one contested view as "the Christian answer."
