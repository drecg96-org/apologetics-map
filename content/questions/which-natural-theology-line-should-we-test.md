# Question

A generic statement such as "the arguments for God do not convince me" is too broad for useful analysis.

M3 fans the discussion into:

- contingency and ultimate explanation;
- cosmic beginning;
- fine-tuning;
- consciousness;
- cognitive reliability and reason;
- morality;
- transcendental arguments;
- and religious experience.

Each branch should be judged by its own premises and its own actual conclusion.
