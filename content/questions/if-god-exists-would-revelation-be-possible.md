# Question

This is the explicit bridge that a lot of apologetics skips.

Even after a positive natural-theology case, a reader should be able to say:

> Fine. Suppose some personal God exists. Why think that God revealed anything?

That question starts the move from philosophy of God to competing historical revelation claims.
