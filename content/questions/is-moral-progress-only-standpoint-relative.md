# Crux question

Take a reform you regard as genuine moral progress.

Is the claim "this was progress" true only relative to a present evaluative standpoint, or was the earlier practice really worse even when the people and institutions supporting it approved of it?

## If progress is standpoint-relative

The relativist can retain meaningful criticism and reform, but the claim remains indexed to an evaluative standpoint. That is a coherent position; it should be recorded rather than treated as a contradiction.

## If progress crosses standpoints

If both the old and new social standards are answerable to a standard that can judge either one mistaken, the conversation has moved toward some form of objectivity.
