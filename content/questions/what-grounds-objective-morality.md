# Question

Suppose at least some moral claims are objectively true. What makes those claims true, and what explains the authority of moral obligations?

This is a grounding question. It is different from asking how people discover moral truths, why people feel motivated to act morally, or whether religious people behave better.

## Main routes

The live options include theistic accounts, non-theistic moral realism, naturalistic accounts tied to facts such as flourishing, constructivist accounts, and anti-realist views that deny stance-independent moral facts altogether.

The moral argument for God is one proposed answer to this question, not the only answer.
