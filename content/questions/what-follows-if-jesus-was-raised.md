# Question

If the conclusion is specifically:

> God raised Jesus from the dead,

then the event would strongly favor the claim that God vindicated Jesus.

That would be a major Christianity-specific result.

But it would still leave downstream questions about exactly who Jesus is, which texts are authoritative, how later doctrines are derived, and how Christianity compares with rival revelation claims.

The branch therefore ends by distinguishing **major confirmation** from **total doctrinal proof**.
