# Question

The Qur'an clearly rejects saying "Three," divine sonship, and the elevation of Jesus from messenger to God.

A more precise historical-theological question is whether every Qur'anic polemic maps exactly onto later Nicene technical distinctions.

That issue should not be used to evade the deeper disagreement.

Even if the Qur'an sometimes addresses a conception unlike a theologian's preferred formulation, Islam and Nicene Christianity still disagree fundamentally about whether Jesus shares in divine identity.
