# Question

M4 separates several objections that are often blurred together:

- the evidential problem of evil;
- natural and animal suffering;
- free will, omnipotence, and the Fall;
- divine hiddenness and sincere nonbelief;
- foreknowledge and freedom;
- why the cross or atonement is needed;
- whether salvation conditions are fair;
- and whether hell is proportionate or everlasting.

A response to one does not automatically answer the others.
