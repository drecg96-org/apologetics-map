# Question

Some goods appear impossible in a world without danger, difficulty, or vulnerability.

There can be no courage without danger, no costly compassion without need, and no mature perseverance without difficulty.

The harder question is whether this explains **the actual scale and distribution** of suffering rather than merely why a perfectly safe world might lack some goods.
