# Response

The Christian counter is not that Judaism lacked all heavenly agents.

It is that the **kind and intensity of devotional inclusion** given to Jesus arose inside a movement whose inherited monotheism sharply regulated worship.

That makes "a merely created but worshiped supreme agent" an explanation with its own cost.

## Remaining issue

The historical fact of extraordinary devotion still does not uniquely determine a later philosophical model of Trinity and incarnation.
