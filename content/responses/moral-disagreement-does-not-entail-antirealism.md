# Response

The existence of disagreement does not logically show that there is no fact of the matter. Disagreement occurs in many domains where realism is not in doubt.

The more serious question is explanatory: is the *kind* of moral disagreement we observe better explained by realism or anti-realism?

Realists can point to factual disagreement, differing non-moral beliefs, unequal information, bias, incentives, and gradual convergence on some norms. Anti-realists can reply that these explanations do not account for the full depth of disagreement.

## Bottom line

This response blocks a quick inference from disagreement to subjectivism. It does not dissolve the broader explanatory challenge.
