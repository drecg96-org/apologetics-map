# Response

Muslim thought has not always meant the same thing by **tahrif**.

At minimum the comparison should distinguish:

- **textual alteration** — the wording of earlier scripture was materially changed;
- **interpretive distortion** — the text remained substantially available but was misread, concealed, or misapplied;
- mixtures of the two.

That distinction matters because each version makes different historical predictions.

A claim of interpretive distortion is compatible with substantial textual continuity. A claim of major textual replacement requires evidence about manuscript history and the timing of the alleged change.
