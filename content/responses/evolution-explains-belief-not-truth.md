# Response

A genealogy of a belief and the truth of that belief are different questions. Explaining why humans came to believe that cooperation matters does not, by itself, show that no moral fact about cooperation exists.

The debunking argument gains force only if the evolutionary story gives a positive reason to think our moral judgments are insensitive to or disconnected from moral truth.

## Theistic and secular replies differ

A theist may argue that evolutionary processes can be providentially ordered toward generally reliable moral cognition. A secular realist needs a different account of why evolved evaluative faculties are sufficiently truth-tracking.

Neither reply is automatic. The central issue is reliability, not merely evolutionary origin.
