# Response

Christianity does not claim that every messianic expectation was visibly completed during Jesus' first-century ministry.

Its answer is an **inaugurated / consummated** pattern:

- Jesus' death and resurrection inaugurate the decisive act;
- his exaltation establishes messianic rule;
- the mission to the nations occurs in the interval;
- resurrection, judgment, peace, and final restoration are consummated at his return.

The central objection is whether this two-stage pattern was genuinely warranted by the relevant texts and events or adopted after expectations failed.
