# Response

A Christian defense should not try to prove too much with a logical model.

If a trinitarian account can coherently distinguish **one divine being** from **three persons**, that may answer the charge of explicit contradiction or crude tritheism.

But coherence supplies only a permission to believe, not a sufficient reason to believe.

The positive case still has to come from the relevant historical, scriptural, and theological evidence about Jesus, the Spirit, and the identity of Israel's God.
