# Response

The right question is not whether morality and epistemology are identical. They plainly are not.

The question is whether the **specific feature** used to reject moral normativity also appears in epistemic normativity. For example, if the objection is that categorical normative authority is metaphysically too strange, and epistemic reasons possess the same kind of authority, the analogy has force unless a relevant difference is identified.

If the anti-realist instead gives a domain-specific objection to morality, the companions-in-guilt argument may have little leverage.

## Status of the dispute

This is a live philosophical debate rather than a clean terminal proof. The map should therefore mark the branch unresolved unless a later node addresses a more specific proposed disanalogy.
