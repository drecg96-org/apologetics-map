# Response

A defender need not picture hell as one identical infinite sentence for every finite act.

Two moves are available:

- accountability may vary by knowledge and culpability;
- separation may concern a continuing orientation that remains opposed to God rather than punishment only for a completed finite act.

## Limit

This reply shifts rather than eliminates the pressure.

It now owes an account of why the orientation becomes permanent and why opportunities for repentance cannot continue.
