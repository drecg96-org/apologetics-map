# Response

Classical Christianity does **not** say:

> three gods = one god.

It says there is one divine being or essence and three personally distinct subjects or persons.

Whether any particular philosophical model succeeds is disputed, but the doctrine should be tested in its strongest actual form rather than as a numerical contradiction it explicitly denies.

## Limit

Logical coherence would not show that God is triune. That requires positive revelatory and historical warrant.
