# Response

The New Testament contains resources for a non-simple-replacement reading.

Jeremiah's new covenant is made **with Israel and Judah**. Paul says God has not rejected his people and warns Gentile Christians not to boast as though they replaced the root that supports them.

## Remaining dispute

This does not settle what continuing Torah observance, land promises, national restoration, or distinct Jewish vocation should mean after Christ.

Christian traditions differ substantially at exactly those points.
