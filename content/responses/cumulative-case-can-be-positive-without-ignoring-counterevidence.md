# Response

Counterevidence matters, but its existence does not logically erase positive evidence.

A responsible cumulative case asks which worldview best fits the **total evidence** after both favorable and unfavorable considerations are included.

M4 exists precisely because the negative evidence deserves deep treatment rather than a one-line dismissal.
