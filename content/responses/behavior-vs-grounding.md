# Response

Concede the behavioral point.

A person does not need to believe in the source of a fact in order to know or respond to that fact. The moral argument therefore should not be framed as a claim about atheists' character.

The live disagreement is whether objective moral facts and obligations require a transcendent ground, can exist as non-theistic necessary facts, can be grounded in natural facts, or should be rejected.

This distinction makes the conversation both more accurate and less needlessly personal.
