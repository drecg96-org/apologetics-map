# Response

Trinitarian Christianity does not picture God as seizing an unrelated innocent person.

The one acting in Christ is God's own self-giving action, and Jesus' death is presented as voluntary.

## Remaining issue

Voluntariness and divine identity answer some versions of the objection but do not automatically show that **punishment can be transferred**. Penal accounts still owe a specific theory of representation, union, or imputation.
