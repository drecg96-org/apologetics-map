# Response

The safest conclusion is not that transcendental reasoning is irrelevant to the choice among religions. It is that **the Christian-specific conclusion should not be hidden inside the opening premise.**

TAG can ask which worldview best accounts for the conditions of rational thought and expose internal tensions in a rival worldview when those tensions bear directly on knowledge, logic, or intelligibility.

But Christianity also makes public claims that can be investigated more directly.

## High-leverage discriminators

If the comparison has narrowed to Christianity, Judaism, and Islam, the map should next test Jesus as Messiah, the crucifixion and resurrection, Jesus' divine identity and the Trinity, covenant continuity, later revelation, and the historical proximity and transmission of the relevant sources.

This creates a cumulative route:

**intelligibility → worldview comparison → theism/Christian structure → historical and revelatory discrimination.**

That makes each inferential step visible and testable.
