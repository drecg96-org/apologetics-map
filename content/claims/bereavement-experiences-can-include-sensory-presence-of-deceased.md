# Claim

A person can sincerely experience the presence, voice, image, or other apparent sensory contact of someone who has died.

## Relevance

This blocks a false dichotomy:

> Either the witness lied, or the dead person objectively appeared.

There are psychologically ordinary categories in between.

## Limit

The existence of a general category does not identify the cause of the particular early Christian reports.
