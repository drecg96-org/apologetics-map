# Claim

There is a large difference between:

> God could not possibly forgive unless blood was shed,

and:

> Given God's chosen purposes of incarnation, reconciliation, justice, victory, and self-giving love, the cross is the fitting or appointed means.

M4 keeps those claims separate.
