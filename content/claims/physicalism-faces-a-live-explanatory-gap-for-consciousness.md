# Claim

There remains a substantive philosophical problem about why and how physical processes are accompanied by phenomenal experience.

## What this does not mean

It does not mean neuroscience has failed, that physicalism has been refuted, or that theism follows.

The claim is only that consciousness remains a live point of metaphysical explanatory pressure.
