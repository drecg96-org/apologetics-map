# Position

A skeptic may simply say:

> "I do not currently believe that God exists."

That is weaker than:

> "I believe that God does not exist."

The distinction matters because the first position may rest mainly on an alleged failure of the available evidence to justify belief. It does not automatically incur the same burden as a positive argument for God's nonexistence.

The map should therefore ask what evidence the person regards as missing, weak, defeated, or insufficient rather than immediately demanding a proof of atheism.
