# Claim

The Christian abductive conclusion is:

> Given the strongest historically defensible data and an explanatory framework in which divine action is a live possibility, Jesus' resurrection is the best overall explanation.

That conclusion is stronger than saying early Christians believed in resurrection and weaker than saying historical method mechanically proves the miracle.

## What has to succeed

The conclusion depends on at least three judgments:

1. the proposed historical data are sufficiently well supported;
2. naturalistic alternatives have meaningful explanatory shortcomings;
3. a resurrection is not excluded in advance by the method or assigned a prior probability so low that no realistic historical evidence could overcome it.

A critic may therefore grant much of the historical core while rejecting the abductive inference.
