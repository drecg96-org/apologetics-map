# Claim

The cumulative case changes what deserves investigation; it does not choose the winner in advance.

Christianity must now compete with:

- Judaism;
- Islam;
- deism or no-special-revelation theism;
- and other relevant revelation claims.

M5 deepens that comparison. M3's job is to make the bridge explicit and auditable.
