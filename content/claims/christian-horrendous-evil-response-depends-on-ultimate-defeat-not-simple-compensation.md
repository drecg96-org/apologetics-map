# Claim

On this approach, an afterlife containing enough pleasure is not automatically an answer to horror.

The stronger Christian claim is that participation in divine life can eventually defeat the horror's power to make the sufferer's life a bad thing overall.

## Limit

This is deeply tradition-specific and depends on Christian eschatological claims that a skeptic need not grant. It also does not make the original suffering instrumentally necessary.
