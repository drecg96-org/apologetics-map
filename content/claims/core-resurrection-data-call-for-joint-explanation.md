# Claim

The relevant historical claims are not independent puzzles if they belong to the same sequence of events.

A satisfactory explanation should therefore ask how well one account handles the **joint pattern**:

- Jesus died;
- some followers had experiences or reports interpreted as appearances;
- resurrection proclamation emerged very early.

## Methodological caution

Joint explanation can favor a unified hypothesis, but simplicity alone is not enough.

A theory must also be plausible against background knowledge, fit the sources, avoid ad hoc rescue assumptions, and compare well with serious rivals.
