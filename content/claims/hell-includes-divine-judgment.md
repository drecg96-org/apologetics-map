# Claim

Any account that says hell is **only** something people do to themselves risks flattening important biblical judgment language.

Christian Scripture repeatedly depicts God as judge. Final condemnation is presented as a verdict with consequences, not merely as an unnoticed psychological state that happens automatically.

## Important limit

This does not by itself settle:

- what the imagery of fire means;
- whether punishment is everlasting conscious suffering, destruction, or something else;
- whether every instance of nonbelief is culpable;
- how divine judgment relates to human freedom.

Those are separate disputes.

The narrow point is that the **judicial dimension should not be erased** when presenting the Christian doctrine.
