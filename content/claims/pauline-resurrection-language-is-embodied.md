# Claim

Paul's resurrection language is not naturally reduced to:

> Jesus' soul survived death while his corpse remained irrelevant.

In 1 Corinthians 15 Paul speaks of resurrection as transformation into a new kind of embodied life.

## Dispute

Scholars disagree over how materially continuous Paul's resurrection body is with the mortal body and how the pre-Pauline formula should be interpreted.

The map should therefore distinguish **embodied resurrection language** from a much stronger claim about exactly what happened to Jesus' corpse.
