# Claim

Logical pluralism is a serious position in contemporary philosophy of logic, not merely the claim that people may choose arbitrary rules.

Different pluralist theories argue that more than one consequence relation can be correct under legitimate interpretations of validity or logical consequence.

## Relevance to TAG

A transcendental argument can still argue that objective logical structure requires a metaphysical ground.

But it should first state which logical facts are doing the work and why the proposed ground explains them. The existence of live pluralist theories prevents the argument from treating a single, uncontested package called "the laws of logic" as a neutral starting point.

## Limit

Logical pluralism is itself contested and does not imply relativism, conventionalism, or atheism.
