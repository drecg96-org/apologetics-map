# Debate gate

This node is a **stage transition**, not an additional proof.

It represents the point where the conversation provisionally grants moral realism strongly enough to ask a new question:

> If objective moral reality exists, what explains or grounds it?

A person can arrive here because they were independently convinced by realism, because one or more realist arguments persuaded them, or simply because they are willing to grant realism for the sake of examining the grounding debate.

## Why the gate matters

Without this transition, the map can visually blur two different disputes:

1. whether objective morality exists;
2. what would ground it if it exists.

The grounding stage should compare theism with secular realist alternatives rather than treating the existence of objective morality itself as evidence that theism has already won.
