# Claim

Even after bracketing the most disputed Gospel details, there remains a historical problem to explain:

- Jesus was killed by crucifixion;
- resurrection proclamation arose early;
- and at least some followers sincerely reported experiences interpreted as encounters with the risen Jesus.

## Limit

Calling this an explanandum does not favor a supernatural explanation by itself. Skeptical and Christian models agree that an origin story is needed and disagree about which model best accounts for it.
