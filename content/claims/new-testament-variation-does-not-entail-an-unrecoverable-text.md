# Claim

The existence of textual variants is not equivalent to the disappearance of the underlying text.

New Testament textual criticism exists because surviving witnesses can be compared. The modern scholarly goal is usually framed more cautiously than recovering a metaphysically guaranteed "perfect original": it is to establish the earliest attainable or initial text for each passage.

## Limit

This does not prove inspiration, inerrancy, or the truth of the events narrated.

It answers the narrower historical question of whether manuscript variation by itself makes the New Testament text inaccessible.
