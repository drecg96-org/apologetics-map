# Intentional unresolved endpoint

A careful reader can grant much of the same historical evidence as an interlocutor and still disagree because they assign different justified weight to:

- natural regularity;
- the prior probability of God;
- the probability that God would act in this way;
- the evidential reliability of the reports;
- and whether historical explanation may name supernatural agency.

M2 does not manufacture a fake consensus here.

The disagreement is explicit so later work on natural theology and worldview comparison can connect back to the actual crux.
