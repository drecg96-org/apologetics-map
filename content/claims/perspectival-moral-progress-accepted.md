# Accepted commitment

On this view, a society can be condemned and its later reforms can count as progress **from an evaluative standpoint** without there being one stance-independent moral scale against which all societies are measured.

This is not "anything goes." A relativist may still appeal to internal coherence, human needs, social cooperation, flourishing, or other constraints. The commitment is narrower: those standards do not amount to a single stance-independent moral order.

## Debate consequence

Do not keep recycling the moral-progress argument after this point. The person has accepted its perspectival interpretation. Pivot to a different line such as categorical reasons, moral fallibility, or epistemic normativity.
