# Claim

A critic may reasonably accept:

- crucifixion;
- early resurrection belief;
- sincere appearance experiences;
- and perhaps even burial or an empty tomb;

while still denying that the evidence **requires** the conclusion that God raised Jesus.

That position can rest on live naturalistic alternatives, agnosticism about disputed data, or a methodological restriction on supernatural causal claims.

## Limit

"Not required" is weaker than "false." This claim leaves open whether resurrection might nevertheless be the best explanation under a broader evidential framework.
