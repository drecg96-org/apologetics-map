# Claim

Skeptical theism can reduce the force of a specific inference:

> I cannot see a sufficient reason → probably there is no sufficient reason.

## Limit

It does not follow that the overall evidential problem disappears. The sheer pattern of suffering, independent likelihood arguments, and worries about skeptical spillover can remain.
