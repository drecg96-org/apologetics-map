# Claim

The key explanatory premise is not simply that **everything has a cause**.

A more careful version says that contingent reality should have an adequate explanation and that a chain consisting only of contingent explananda does not yet supply an ultimate stopping point.

## Main dispute

A critic may accept contingent facts as brute, deny that the totality needs an additional explanation, or argue that explanatory demands are context-sensitive rather than governed by a universal Principle of Sufficient Reason.
