# Claim

No single naturalistic explanation is cost-free.

Different proposals can explain different portions of the evidence:

- visionary or bereavement experiences can explain some appearance reports;
- legendary development can explain later narrative elaboration;
- deception can explain deliberate testimony if motivation and opportunity are supplied;
- body-removal theories can explain an empty tomb if one grants it;
- mixed theories can combine several mechanisms.

The affirmative resurrection case argues that such accounts either leave important facts unexplained or accumulate independent assumptions.

## Why this remains contested

A critic can reasonably answer that historical explanations often are multi-causal, that natural mechanisms enjoy stronger background plausibility, or that the data are too uncertain to demand one unified cause.

This premise must therefore be earned by comparing alternatives rather than asserted as "all natural explanations fail."
