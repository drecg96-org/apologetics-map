# Claim

A careful fine-tuning argument is comparative.

It asks whether the observed life-permitting conditions would be more expected if reality has a purposive source than under specified non-purposive alternatives.

## Why this remains contested

The comparison depends on disputed probability distributions, selection effects, multiverse proposals, the possibility of deeper physical necessity, and what a designer would be expected to value or create.
