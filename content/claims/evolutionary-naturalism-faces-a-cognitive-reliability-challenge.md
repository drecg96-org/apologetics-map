# Claim

The challenge is conditional.

If a worldview gives its adherent an undefeated reason to distrust the general reliability of the cognitive faculties that produced that worldview belief, the position threatens to become self-undermining.

## What remains disputed

Critics deny that naturalism plus evolution makes reliability unlikely, deny the move from inscrutable conditional probability to a defeater, or argue that independent grounds for trust in cognition survive the argument.
