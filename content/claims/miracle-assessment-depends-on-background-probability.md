# Claim

The same historical reports can rationally be weighted differently by people with different justified background models.

If divine action is judged virtually impossible, resurrection begins with an extremely low prior. If theism and purposive divine action already have non-negligible probability, the prior can be materially higher.

## Why this matters

The resurrection debate cannot be reduced to counting documents. A major crux lies upstream in background worldview probability and the rules used to combine it with the case-specific evidence.

This does not mean "everyone gets their own truth." Background assumptions themselves are open to argument.
