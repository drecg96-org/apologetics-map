# Claim

The immediate conclusion of a contingency argument is deliberately narrow:

> Some necessary or non-contingent ground ultimately explains contingent reality.

## What does not yet follow

This conclusion by itself does not establish that the ground is:

- personal;
- omniscient;
- morally perfect;
- triune;
- or the God revealed in Christianity.

Those attributes require additional argument.
