# Claim

A naturalistic account need not say the disciples were frauds.

It can instead propose:

1. genuine post-bereavement or religious experiences;
2. interpretation of those experiences through Jewish and scriptural categories;
3. social transmission of the conviction that Jesus was alive and vindicated;
4. and later narrative development of appearance and tomb traditions.

## Main pressure

This model must still explain the specific shape and breadth of the traditions, the early bodily-resurrection language, and—if granted—the empty tomb.
