# Claim

Cumulative reasoning is not a machine that turns several contested arguments into certainty.

A skeptic can reasonably argue that:

- the lines share premises rather than accumulating independently;
- rival explanations remain live;
- theism's greater scope brings additional commitments;
- and evil, hiddenness, or religious diversity supply counterevidence.

The map preserves that endpoint rather than treating continued dissent as a navigation error.
