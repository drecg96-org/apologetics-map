# Claim

Religious experience need not be treated as either conclusive revelation or evidentially worthless.

A modest position is that an apparently veridical experience can count as **some evidence** unless counterevidence, unreliability, conflicting interpretation, or another defeater substantially weakens it.

## Limit

Religious diversity and psychological alternatives make the move from generic transcendence to one tradition especially difficult.
