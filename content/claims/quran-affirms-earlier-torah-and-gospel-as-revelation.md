# Claim

The Qur'an does not speak of earlier Jewish and Christian revelation only as falsehood.

In Al-Ma'idah 5:44-48 it describes the Torah and Gospel as revelation containing guidance and light, tells the People of the Gospel to judge by what God revealed there, and presents the Qur'an as confirming earlier scripture while also functioning as a criterion over it.

## Comparative significance

This creates a real interpretive burden for both simplistic poles.

A Christian critique cannot fairly say Islam simply rejects all earlier revelation. A Muslim defense cannot simply assume that "the Bible was corrupted" settles every conflict without specifying what kind of corruption is claimed, when it occurred, and what evidence supports that claim.
