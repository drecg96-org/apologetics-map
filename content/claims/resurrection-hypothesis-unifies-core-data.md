# Claim

If Jesus really was raised from death, several central resurrection data would receive one unified explanation:

- death by crucifixion is followed by renewed life rather than survival from a merely apparent death;
- appearance experiences are caused by the risen Jesus rather than generated independently;
- and early resurrection proclamation arises because the event actually occurred.

## What this does not establish

A hypothesis explaining the data **if true** is not thereby the best explanation.

The difficult questions are whether a supernatural resurrection has an acceptable prior plausibility, whether the historical data are strong enough, and whether natural alternatives can explain the pattern with fewer costs.
