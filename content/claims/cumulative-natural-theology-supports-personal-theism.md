# Claim

The cumulative conclusion is intentionally stronger than the conclusion of any one natural-theology argument but weaker than Christianity.

If several lines survive their best objections, they can jointly support something like:

> A necessary or transcendent, rational, purposive, mental or personal, morally significant source of reality is a serious explanatory hypothesis.

## Limit

This still leaves open deism, non-Christian monotheism, some forms of idealism, and disputes over classical divine attributes.
