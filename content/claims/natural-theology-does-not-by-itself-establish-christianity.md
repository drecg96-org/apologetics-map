# Claim

This is the central M3 bridge rule:

> God exists

does not entail:

> Christianity is true.

A person can rationally move toward theism while remaining unconvinced by Christianity, Judaism, Islam, or any claim of special revelation.

The next stage must test revelation rather than assume it.
