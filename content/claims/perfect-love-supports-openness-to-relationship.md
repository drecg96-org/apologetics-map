# Claim

The hiddenness argument begins from a relational expectation, not merely a demand for spectacular evidence.

If God is perfectly loving and seeks relationship, nonresistant persons should not be indefinitely prevented from the minimum epistemic conditions required for conscious reciprocal relationship.
