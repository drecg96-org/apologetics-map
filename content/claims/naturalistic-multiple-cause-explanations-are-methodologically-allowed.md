# Claim

History does not require a single cause to explain every part of a complex event sequence.

A critic may therefore combine, for example:

- grief or visionary experience for some appearance claims;
- ordinary tradition development for later details;
- uncertainty about burial or tomb history;
- and social reinforcement for persistence of belief.

## Constraint

A mixed explanation is not automatically better merely because every component is natural. It pays a complexity cost and each component needs independent plausibility.
