# Position

This is a stronger skeptical claim:

> "The evidence makes God's existence unlikely," or "God does not exist."

Now positive arguments matter directly. The person may appeal to evil, hiddenness, low prior probability, explanatory comparison, incoherence in a particular concept of God, or some combination of considerations.

A fair debate should identify the actual argument rather than treating every atheist as though they were merely saying "there is no evidence."
