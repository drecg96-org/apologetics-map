# Claim

A worldview in which mentality is fundamental has one prima facie explanatory advantage: consciousness is not required to emerge from a wholly nonmental base.

## Crucial qualification

That advantage is **not uniquely theistic**.

Panpsychism, idealism, and some dualist views can also treat mentality as fundamental. A theistic argument therefore needs a comparative case, not merely an anti-physicalist gap.
