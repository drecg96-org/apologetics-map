# Claim

A necessary ground is not automatically a personal designer.

A designer is not automatically morally perfect.

A moral ground is not automatically the Trinity.

A challenge to naturalism is not automatically evidence for Christianity.

M3 treats these as **partial conclusions** that can later be synthesized without pretending that any one line has done all the work.
