# Claim

A cumulative case gains strength only to the extent that its lines contribute genuinely additional information.

Several arguments may share:

- a preference for explanation over brute fact;
- similar priors about theism;
- overlapping intuitions about necessity or normativity;
- or the same disputed inference-to-best-explanation framework.

M3 therefore treats evidential dependence as part of the argument rather than assuming every line adds a full independent unit of support.
