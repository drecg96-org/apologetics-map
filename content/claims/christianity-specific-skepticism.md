# Position

Rejecting Christianity is not the same as rejecting every possible God.

Someone may grant that the universe has a creator, that objective morality exists, or even that religious experience can be meaningful while still doubting the resurrection, biblical reliability, Christian exclusivity, or particular doctrines.

This branch prevents arguments for generic theism from being treated as though they already establish Christianity.
