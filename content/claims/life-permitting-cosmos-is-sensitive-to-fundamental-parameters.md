# Claim

The fine-tuning discussion begins with the observation that life-permitting conditions depend sensitively on features of the universe.

## What this does not establish

Sensitivity is not yet design.

The argument still needs a probability model and comparison class: design, single-universe chance, deeper necessity, multiverse selection, or some presently unknown physics.
