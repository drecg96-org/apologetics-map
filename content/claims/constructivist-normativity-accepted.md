# Accepted commitment

This position rejects the idea that current preferences automatically make moral judgments true, while also rejecting the need for a realm of stance-independent moral facts.

Normative authority is instead explained through rational construction: what follows from the standards of deliberation, agency, or an appropriately specified evaluative standpoint.

## Debate consequence

At this point "but your present opinion could be wrong" no longer addresses the theory. The live questions concern whether the proposed procedure is non-circular, whether its standards are genuinely binding, and whether competing rational procedures can yield incompatible outputs.
