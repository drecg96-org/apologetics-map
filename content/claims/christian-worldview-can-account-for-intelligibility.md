# Claim

Christianity contains an internally available explanation for why rational creatures inhabit an intelligible world.

On this account:

- reality is created rather than ultimately chaotic;
- the Creator is rational and faithful rather than arbitrary;
- human beings are finite image-bearers rather than accidental sources of normativity;
- creation has stable order;
- and revelation makes creaturely knowledge possible without requiring omniscience.

## What this establishes

At most, this shows that Christianity has **resources** for an account of intelligibility.

It does not yet show that those resources are uniquely successful, that every Christian formulation avoids circularity, or that rival worldviews fail.
