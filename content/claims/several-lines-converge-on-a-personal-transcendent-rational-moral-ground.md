# Claim

The cumulative case is not merely "many arguments therefore more confidence."

Its stronger form asks whether the **different target properties line up**:

- contingency → necessary or non-contingent ground;
- fine-tuning → purposive intelligence;
- consciousness → fundamental mentality;
- morality → necessarily good personal ground;
- reason → a worldview friendly to intelligibility and cognitive trust.

If those arguments succeed, their conclusions overlap in a way that is closer to personal theism than any one argument reaches alone.
