# Claim

Paul does not present the entire resurrection sequence in 1 Corinthians 15 as a fresh invention. He says he delivered what he had received.

## Why it matters

That pushes at least some resurrection proclamation and named appearance tradition behind the date of the letter itself.

## Limit

The text does not supply a calendar date for when Paul received the material, and scholars differ over exactly how much of verses 3–7 belongs to the earlier tradition.
