# Claim

There was no single first-century Jewish checklist that every group applied identically to a coming Messiah.

Royal Davidic restoration was important, but Jewish sources also exhibit priestly, prophetic, apocalyptic, heavenly, and sometimes multiple-messiah expectations.

## Limit

Diversity does not make every messianic claimant equally plausible.

It prevents the debate from simply importing one later formulation into the first century and treating deviation from that formulation as historically decisive.
