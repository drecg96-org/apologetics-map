# Claim

At least some of Jesus' earliest followers had experiences or sincerely made reports that they interpreted as appearances of Jesus after his death.

The claim is deliberately narrower than:

> Jesus physically appeared after being supernaturally raised.

A historian can grant the existence and sincerity of appearance experiences while explaining them as visions, bereavement phenomena, religious experiences, memory development, or some combination.

## Why this distinction matters

If the map calls the experiences themselves "the resurrection," it assumes the disputed explanation inside the datum.

The cause and ontological status of the experiences belong later in the argument.
