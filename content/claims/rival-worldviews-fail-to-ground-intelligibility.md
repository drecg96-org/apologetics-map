# Claim

The strongest form of TAG requires more than showing that Christianity can tell a coherent story about reason.

It additionally claims that relevant rival worldviews **cannot** supply an adequate account of the same conditions without inconsistency, arbitrariness, or borrowing resources unavailable on their own terms.

## Why this is the hardest premise

A naturalist, Platonist, Muslim, Jew, idealist, or other rival need not accept the Christian description of their worldview's failure.

The claim therefore has to be established by concrete comparison. It cannot simply be stipulated by defining every non-Christian position as "autonomous" and then declaring autonomy unintelligible.

This premise bears much of the argumentative weight in a Christian-specific TAG.
