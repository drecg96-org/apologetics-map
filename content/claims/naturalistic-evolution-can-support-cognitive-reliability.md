# Claim

A naturalist can argue that sufficiently accurate representation of the environment often contributes to adaptive behavior.

That gives evolutionary theory a route toward explaining why perception, memory, and reasoning are reliable enough for successful action and inquiry.

## Limit

This does not automatically explain every abstract or metaphysical belief, nor does it settle debates about semantic content, higher reasoning, or global epistemic trust.
