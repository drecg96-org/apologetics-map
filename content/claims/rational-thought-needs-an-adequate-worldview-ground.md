# Claim

A worldview that relies on rational inference while making rational inference unintelligible on its own terms faces an internal problem.

This does not mean every worldview must provide a complete theory of logic, semantics, cognition, and metaphysics before anyone may reason. The more limited claim is that the worldview's deeper commitments should be compatible with—and ideally help explain—the conditions it repeatedly presupposes.

## Why this is an intermediate claim

This is the bridge from the starting datum **we reason** to the comparative question **which worldview can account for reasoning?**

It still does not identify Christianity as the answer.
