# Claim

The historical picture is more precise than either "no variation ever existed" or "the Qur'an was radically rewritten."

Academic treatments discuss:

- early collection and redaction;
- an Uthmanic consonantal standard;
- non-canonical and canonical readings;
- and the later stabilization of recognized recitation traditions.

## Comparative significance

This can support a strong claim of early textual stabilization without turning textual history itself into an argument for divine authorship.
