# Claim

If the historical conclusion were not merely "the disciples believed Jesus was alive" but **God actually raised Jesus**, the event would carry theological significance.

For Christianity, resurrection is not an isolated oddity. It is divine vindication of Jesus.

## What follows

It would substantially increase the credibility of Jesus' mission and the early apostolic claim that God acted uniquely in him.

## What does not automatically follow

It would not by itself settle every later question about canon, Trinity, atonement theory, church authority, or the interpretation of every biblical text. Those require additional arguments.
