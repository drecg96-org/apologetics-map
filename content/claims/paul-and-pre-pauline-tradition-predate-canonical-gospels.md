# Claim

The earliest recoverable resurrection proclamation is not first found in the canonical Gospel narratives. Paul is earlier, and 1 Corinthians 15 contains material he says he received.

## Why it matters

A "centuries later legend" explanation is historically inadequate. Any naturalistic account has to explain why resurrection belief and appearance tradition are already present at an early stage.

## Limit

Early proximity constrains some forms of legendary-development theory. It does not make error, visionary experience, theological interpretation, or shorter-term tradition development impossible.
