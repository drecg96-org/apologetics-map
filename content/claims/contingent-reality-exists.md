# Claim

The contingency argument starts modestly: at least some things exist that do not appear metaphysically necessary.

This premise is weaker than saying the entire universe is contingent and much weaker than saying every fact must have an explanation.

## Why it matters

The next dispute is whether contingent reality as a whole requires an explanation beyond further contingent facts.
