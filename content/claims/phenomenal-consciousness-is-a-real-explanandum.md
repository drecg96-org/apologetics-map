# Claim

Whatever theory one prefers, conscious experience is not optional data.

An adequate worldview must account for the existence, character, and apparent causal role of subjective experience.

## Limit

This starting point is neutral between physicalism, dualism, idealism, panpsychism, theism, and other metaphysical views.
