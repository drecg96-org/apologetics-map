# Claim

If ultimate reality is personal rather than impersonal, communication with finite persons is no longer ruled out in principle.

But possibility is not actuality.

A revelation claim still needs evidence about:

- historical proximity;
- source quality;
- internal and external coherence;
- purported signs or miracles;
- continuity with prior revelation;
- and competing claims.
