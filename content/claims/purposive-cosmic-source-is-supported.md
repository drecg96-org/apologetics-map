# Claim

The strongest warranted conclusion is proportional to the argument.

A successful fine-tuning inference would support something like **purposive intelligence relevant to cosmic order**.

It would not yet establish moral perfection, incarnation, Trinity, biblical authority, or even every attribute associated with classical theism.
