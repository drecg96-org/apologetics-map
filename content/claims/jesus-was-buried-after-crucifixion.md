# Claim

Jesus' body was buried after his crucifixion.

This claim is less secure than the bare fact of crucifixion and should not inherit the same confidence label automatically. The more specific claim that Joseph of Arimathea buried Jesus in a known rock-cut tomb requires additional argument.

## Why it matters

Burial creates the historical setting in which an empty-tomb claim can even be evaluated.

## Limit

Burial does not imply that the tomb was later empty, that the burial location remained known, or that Jesus rose.
