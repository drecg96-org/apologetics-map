# Claim

Early Christian narrative traditions report that Jesus' burial place was found empty.

The claim should be kept distinct from two stronger propositions:

1. that an identifiable tomb **was in fact** found empty;
2. that resurrection is the best explanation of an empty tomb.

## Why this wording matters

The existence and content of the narratives are textual facts. Their historical reliability is the disputed inference.

This separation allows evidence about narrative form, witnesses, literary dependence, and burial tradition to be assessed without assuming the conclusion.
