# Claim

A cumulative case is not genuinely cumulative if it adds only favorable evidence.

The same ledger must include serious negative evidence, including:

- apparently gratuitous suffering;
- nonresistant nonbelief;
- religious disagreement and diversity;
- and costs or tensions in proposed divine attributes.

M4 deepens those objections. M3 makes clear now that they belong in the final probability judgment.
