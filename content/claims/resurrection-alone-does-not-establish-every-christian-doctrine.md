# Claim

A resurrection conclusion would be high leverage, not logically exhaustive.

The inference would look more like:

> If God raised Jesus, Christianity receives major confirmation.

not:

> If God raised Jesus, every later Christian doctrine is therefore proven.

## Why this matters

This keeps the map from smuggling an entire worldview into one historical inference. It also creates a clean handoff to later milestones on revelation, Judaism and Islam, Scripture, Trinity, and atonement.
