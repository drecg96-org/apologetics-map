# Claim

Even if a cumulative case points toward personal theism, the map should not quietly import the entire classical doctrine of God.

Claims such as omnipotence, omniscience, perfect goodness, uniqueness, timelessness, and simplicity require their own philosophical development.

This distinction keeps later objections about evil, freedom, and hiddenness attached to explicitly defended divine attributes rather than to an undefined "God."
