# Claim

The resurrection tradition is neither a static verbatim transcript nor a wholly late invention.

There is early continuity: death, being raised, and appearances are already present in Pauline material. There is also later narrative elaboration and variation across the Gospels.

## Why it matters

A skeptical model may use development to explain some later details without denying that resurrection belief began early.

## Limit

Development does not by itself tell us whether the originating belief was true or false.
