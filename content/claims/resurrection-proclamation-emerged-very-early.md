# Claim

The proclamation that Jesus had been raised belongs to the early Christian movement rather than first appearing after a long period in which no resurrection claim existed.

This matters because a purely late-legend theory must account for why resurrection belief appears so near the movement's beginnings.

## Limit

Early belief is evidence about **what early Christians believed and proclaimed**.

Its early date does not by itself prove that the belief was true.
