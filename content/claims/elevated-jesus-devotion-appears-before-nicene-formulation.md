# Claim

The historical question should not be framed as though Christians first began treating Jesus as exalted or divine at Nicaea.

High devotional treatment of Jesus appears in sources centuries earlier, including first-century Pauline material.

## What this establishes

The phenomenon requiring explanation is early.

## What it does not establish

Early worship or exalted language does not by itself prove the fully developed Nicene doctrine, settle every title's meaning, or show that the earliest Christians all held one identical Christology.
