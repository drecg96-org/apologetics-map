# Evidence

Paul is not merely reporting what unnamed others told him. He explicitly includes **his own** encounter with Jesus in the resurrection-appearance sequence.

He also acknowledges that he had previously persecuted the Jesus movement.

## Why it matters

This gives the historical discussion a first-person source from someone whose allegiance to the movement changed dramatically.

It is difficult to explain Paul's participation by saying he merely inherited a belief he had always wanted to hold.

## What this does not establish

Sincerity and personal transformation do not determine the cause of the experience. Visions, revelations, neurological events, religious experiences, and an external risen Jesus remain competing interpretations.

## Main challenges

Paul's experience may differ phenomenologically from whatever Cephas or the Twelve experienced. Treating every item in the appearance list as the same kind of event requires argument.
