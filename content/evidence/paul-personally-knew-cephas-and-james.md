# Evidence

Paul says that after joining the movement he spent time with **Cephas** and met **James**.

Both names occur in the resurrection appearance tradition Paul later preserves in 1 Corinthians 15.

## Why it matters

The author transmitting the appearance list was not separated from every named early witness by several anonymous generations.

Personal contact does not prove what Cephas or James experienced, but it shortens the chain of transmission for the existence of their claims.

## What this does not establish

Paul does not tell us in Galatians that he interviewed them specifically about every resurrection detail later listed in 1 Corinthians.

## Main challenges

The evidence supports proximity and opportunity for communication, not a transcript of what was discussed.
