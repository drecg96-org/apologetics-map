# Evidence

Paul's undisputed correspondence belongs earlier than the canonical Gospel resurrection narratives. In 1 Corinthians 15, Paul also marks at least part of the material as something he had **received** and then passed on.

## Why it matters

A theory in which resurrection proclamation first appears only after generations of Gospel legend is too late. The historian has to explain an already-existing resurrection and appearance tradition in the Pauline period.

## What this does not establish

Earlier is not the same as true. An early belief can still be mistaken, visionary, interpretive, or otherwise naturally explained.

## Main challenges

The exact date, extent, and original wording of the pre-Pauline material are reconstructed rather than directly timestamped, and different scholars draw the boundaries differently.
