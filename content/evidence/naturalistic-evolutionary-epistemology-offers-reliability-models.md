# Evidence

A sweeping claim that naturalism has **no account at all** of reliable cognition is too strong.

Evolutionary epistemology can argue that organisms whose perceptual and inferential systems track action-relevant structure in the environment often enjoy a selective advantage. This supplies a naturalistic mechanism by which substantial truth-tracking can emerge and persist.

## Why it matters

This is direct counterpressure on the rival-worldview-failure premise.

A defender of TAG must therefore show why evolutionary truth-tracking is insufficient for the relevant kind of rational warrant rather than assuming that natural selection is indifferent to truth in every respect.

## What this does not establish

Evolutionary success does not automatically explain reliability in abstract mathematics, metaphysics, modal reasoning, or every domain remote from ancestral fitness.

The result is a live dispute over the scope and depth of naturalistic reliability, not a simple victory for either side.
