# Evidence

Paul introduces the material in 1 Corinthians 15 by saying that he **received** it and then **delivered** it to the Corinthians.

Scholars commonly treat at least the compact death–burial–raised–appearance sequence in verses 3–5 as traditional material Paul did not originate when writing the letter.

## Why it matters

The resurrection proclamation therefore predates the composition of 1 Corinthians and reaches into an earlier stage of the movement.

That places resurrection belief closer to the movement's origins than a theory of very late invention would predict.

## What this does not establish

A tradition can be early and false. Early transmission does not by itself verify the events described.

## Main challenges

Scholars debate the exact extent of the inherited formula, how early Paul received it, and which phrases reflect Pauline editing.
