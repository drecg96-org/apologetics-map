# Evidence

Paul's appearance list includes a specific appearance to **James**.

James later appears in Paul's own letters as an important Jerusalem leader, which makes the name historically significant rather than an otherwise unknown figure introduced only for the story.

## Why it matters

The datum broadens the appearance tradition beyond Cephas and the Twelve and connects it with another major early leader.

## What this does not establish

The text does not tell us what James experienced or demonstrate that the experience caused his leadership or conversion.

## Main challenges

The extent to which verse 7 is pre-Pauline rather than Pauline composition is debated, and later narratives should not be retrojected into Paul's brief statement.
