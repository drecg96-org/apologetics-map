# Evidence

The canonical resurrection accounts overlap but are not identical in their narrative details.

## Why it matters

Variation is relevant to questions of literary dependence, memory, tradition development, and harmonization. It prevents the debate from treating the later narratives as one undifferentiated eyewitness transcript.

## What this does not establish

Differences in detail do not by themselves show that the core claims are false. Ancient sources can vary while still preserving historically useful information.

## Main challenge

The interpretive dispute concerns whether the differences are ordinary variation around a stable core, evidence of substantial legendary development, or some mixture of both.
