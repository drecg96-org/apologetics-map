# Evidence

The Synoptic resurrection narratives are not three wholly unrelated documents. Matthew and Luke use Mark extensively.

## Why it matters

The mere count "Mark + Matthew + Luke + John = four witnesses" overstates the independence of the canonical evidence.

## What this does not establish

Literary dependence does not imply that every empty-tomb detail in Matthew or Luke comes only from Mark, nor does it determine John's relationship to the Synoptic tradition.

## Main challenge

The historical question becomes source-sensitive: which elements are shared because of literary dependence, which reflect other tradition, and which may be independent?
