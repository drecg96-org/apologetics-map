# Evidence

Bereaved people can have vivid experiences of presence, voice, image, touch, or other sensory-like contact with someone who has died.

## Why it matters

That makes "a sincere postmortem experience occurred" compatible in principle with a natural psychological cause.

## What this does not establish

The evidence is modern and general. It does not tell us which ancient appearance claims were experiences of this kind, whether more than one person was involved, or whether any particular report had an external cause.

## Main challenge

Moving from a known class of modern experiences to a specific explanation of first-century reports requires a separate historical bridge.
