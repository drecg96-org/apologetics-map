# Agent summary

Morriston distinguishes claims about moral truth, moral obligation, and their alleged need for a divine foundation. He argues that proposed theistic explanations face unresolved questions and that secular moral facts cannot simply be dismissed as inadequate stopping points.

## How to use this source

Use as a direct scholarly critique of the grounding step in the moral argument.
