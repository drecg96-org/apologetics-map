# Source

Useful for representing a stricter confessional Christian answer to the good-nonbeliever scenario.

Chapter 10 makes two claims relevant to this branch: salvation is the work of divine grace rather than a reward for foreseen moral merit, and non-Christian moral diligence is not treated as an independent saving route. This is a Reformed confessional position, not a summary of every Christian tradition.
