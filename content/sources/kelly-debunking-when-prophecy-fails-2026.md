# Source profile

This archival reassessment challenges the standard narrative of the group described in *When Prophecy Fails*.

## Why it belongs here

Cognitive-dissonance explanations are often imported into resurrection discussion by analogy. This source is a useful warning against treating one famous modern case as a settled empirical template.

It does not refute cognitive dissonance theory in general or rule out dissonance-related mechanisms in ancient religious movements.
