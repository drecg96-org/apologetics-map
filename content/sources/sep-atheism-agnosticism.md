# Source profile

A useful diagnostic source because it does not assume that every non-theist is making the same claim.

It distinguishes several questions that should remain separate in the map:

- Does a person merely lack belief, or positively judge that God does not exist?
- Are they suspending judgment because they regard the evidence as inconclusive?
- Are they rejecting all divine realities, or only a specific conception of God?
- Are they skeptical of theism in general, or of Christianity in particular?

That taxonomy helps prevent the debate explorer from assigning a stronger claim to a skeptic than the skeptic actually holds.
