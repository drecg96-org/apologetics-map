# Source

Especially useful for the inference from **changing or differing social norms** to **moral relativism**.

The entry distinguishes descriptive moral relativism — the empirical claim that societies exhibit significant moral disagreement — from metaethical moral relativism, the philosophical thesis that moral truth or justification is relative to a person or group. The former does not simply equal the latter, so observed cultural change needs an additional argument before it establishes a metaethical conclusion.
