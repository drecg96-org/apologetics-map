# Source

A neutral map of major Christian and philosophical positions on heaven and hell.

Especially useful here because it distinguishes retributive or judicial accounts from free-will accounts in which hell is understood as a freely embraced condition of separation from God. It also discusses objections to the claim that a person can knowingly and freely choose eternal separation from the source of their own good.
