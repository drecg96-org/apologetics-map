# Source profile

Murphy-O'Connor analyzes tradition and Pauline redaction in 1 Corinthians 15:3–7. He treats verses 3b–5 as a primitive unified creed received and reshaped by Paul and finds traditional material extending into the later appearance list.

## Use

Use this for the textual-historical proposition that Paul is transmitting earlier resurrection tradition rather than composing every element of 1 Corinthians 15 on the spot.

## Limit

Identifying traditional material does not prove the events described in the tradition occurred.
