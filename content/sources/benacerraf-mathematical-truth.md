# Source profile

Benacerraf argues that an adequate philosophy of mathematics must satisfy both semantic and epistemological demands. The paper became a central reference point for the question of how finite knowers could have mathematical knowledge if mathematical objects are abstract and causally isolated.

## How to use this source

Use it as a pressure point against a simple Platonist answer to TAG: abstracta can preserve objectivity and necessity, but doing so raises a separate account-of-knowledge problem.

## Limitation

The paper does not establish that Platonism is incoherent or that theism is required. Platonists have developed multiple responses to the epistemological challenge.
