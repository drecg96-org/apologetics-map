# Agent summary

Van Til identifies the self-attesting Christ of Scripture as his starting point and rejects an apologetic method that treats autonomous human reason as the final court over revelation. He argues by presupposition: the Christian position is claimed to be the necessary foundation for proof, predication, and intelligible experience.

The essay is especially important for the Christianity-specific question because Van Til does not present his method as an inference first to generic theism. The intended foundation is explicitly Christian and Christ-centered.

## How to use this source

Use as a primary source for what Van Til himself claims. Do not use the fact that he states Christian uniqueness as evidence that he has successfully demonstrated it; that is the disputed philosophical step mapped elsewhere.
