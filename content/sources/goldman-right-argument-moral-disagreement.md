# Agent summary

Goldman argues that some persistent moral disagreements are not well explained as ordinary factual error or incomplete information. He develops disagreement as a serious challenge to realism, focusing on conflicts whose resolution seems to require already-contested values rather than a neutral procedure.

## How to use this source

Use as a strong contemporary formulation of the disagreement objection, paired with realist replies rather than as a settled result.
