# Source profile

A neutral overview of Hick's soul-making approach.

The view offers a positive theodicy rather than merely a defense: some risk, struggle, distance, and suffering may be conditions for developing mature virtue. Its obvious pressure point is whether the actual scale of suffering—including horrors and animal pain—is remotely proportionate to that purpose.
