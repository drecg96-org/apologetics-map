# Source profile

Give Me An Answer publishes long-form and short-form campus conversations, debates, and Q&A. The ministry grew out of Cliffe Knechtle's public dialogues with university students; Stuart Knechtle now co-hosts and participates in the campus work.

## Best used for

Common skeptical objections in conversational form: how much evidence is enough for God, why Jesus is unique, suffering and hell, morality, biblical trustworthiness, other religions, doubt, and practical objections to Christian belief.

## Source discipline

These are evangelistic and apologetic conversations rather than neutral scholarship. They are especially valuable for mapping real-world objection-response sequences and phrasing, while factual claims should be traced to stronger primary or academic sources when possible.
