# Agent summary

This chapter surveys the Islamic portrayal of Jesus: miraculous conception and prophetic status are affirmed, divinity is denied, and the passion narrative is reworked around divine deliverance rather than the Christian crucifixion-resurrection sequence.

## How to use this source

Use it as academic context alongside primary Qur'anic passages and Muslim confessional sources. It helps distinguish what the Qur'an itself says from later Islamic developments.
