# Source profile

Ware examines the pre-Pauline formula in 1 Corinthians 15:3–5 and the long-running debate about what kind of resurrection the formula expresses.

## Use

This source is useful for distinguishing two questions:

- whether Paul is transmitting earlier material;
- what that material means by resurrection.

Those should not be collapsed into one evidential claim.

## Limit

Ware argues for a particular interpretation of the resurrection language. That interpretation is part of an active scholarly debate and should not be labeled consensus merely because the formula itself is widely regarded as traditional material.
