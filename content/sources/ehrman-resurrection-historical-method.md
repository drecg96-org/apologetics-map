# Source profile

Ehrman distinguishes questions that historians can investigate from the stronger claim that historical method can prove a supernatural cause.

That distinction is useful in resurrection debates because a skeptic may grant substantial historical data—Jesus' crucifixion, early resurrection belief, experiences interpreted as appearances—while denying that the historian is methodologically entitled to infer "God raised Jesus from the dead."

This source represents Ehrman's methodological objection. It should be paired with historians and philosophers who argue that historical inference can rationally favor a miracle in a sufficiently strong explanatory case.
