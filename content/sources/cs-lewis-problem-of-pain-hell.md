# Source

Lewis is a major modern source for the popular formulation that the doors of hell are, in an important sense, locked from the inside.

Use this as an apologetic formulation of the free-will model, not as though it settles the biblical or philosophical debate by itself. The stronger philosophical question is whether every damned person can fairly be described as knowingly and freely rejecting God.
