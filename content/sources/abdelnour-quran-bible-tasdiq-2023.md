# Source profile

Abdelnour distinguishes textual alteration from interpretive distortion and argues that the Qur'an repeatedly presents itself as confirming earlier revelation.

This is useful for representing an internally Muslim challenge to a simplistic claim that the Qur'an plainly teaches wholesale textual corruption of the Bible.
