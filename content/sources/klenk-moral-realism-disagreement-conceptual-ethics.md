# Agent summary

Klenk distinguishes facts about moral concepts from the metaphysical question whether moral facts exist. This helps explain why disagreement can look deep and genuine without forcing an anti-realist conclusion.

## How to use this source

Use to qualify simplistic disagreement arguments and add a contemporary realist explanation of persistent conflict.
