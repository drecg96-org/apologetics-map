# Source

Important counterexample to the claim that all Christian traditions teach that mere absence of explicit Christian belief automatically entails damnation.

Paragraph 847 allows that a person who lacks knowledge of Christ through no fault of their own may attain salvation if they sincerely seek God and respond to grace as they understand it through conscience.
