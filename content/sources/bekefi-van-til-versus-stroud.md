# Agent summary

Békefi introduces Van Til's transcendental argument for Christian theism, develops the Stroudian objection against ambitious transcendental arguments, and evaluates several Van Tilian responses. He concludes that the response strategies examined face serious problems and offers an additional objection based on tensions with Christian theism itself.
