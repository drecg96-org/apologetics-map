# Source profile

A balanced reference for miracle debates.

It distinguishes the concept of a miracle from the evidence for a particular miracle and surveys both arguments for reported miracles and general or case-specific skeptical arguments against them.

Use it to avoid treating either "extraordinary claims require extraordinary evidence" or "eyewitness testimony proves a miracle" as self-interpreting slogans. The evidential structure has to be made explicit.
