# Source profile

Wright gives a broad historical argument about what "resurrection" meant in ancient Jewish and early Christian settings and argues that the early Christian mutation is best explained by an empty tomb plus appearances.

## How to use this source

Use especially for the semantic and historical question:

> What did early Christians mean when they said Jesus was raised?

It is a major affirmative scholarly case, not a neutral consensus source.
