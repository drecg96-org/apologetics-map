# Agent summary

Murphy treats divine authority as a philosophical problem rather than simply assuming that divine status entails authority. The analysis asks what reasons agents have to comply and how authority can generate or alter obligations.

## How to use this source

Use to deepen the claim that moral obligation has a relational or authoritative structure and to expose the premises that structure requires.
