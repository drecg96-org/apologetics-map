# Source profile

A major Christian treatment of **horrendous evil**.

Adams is valuable because she does not answer horrors by saying that every instance is obviously instrumentally necessary for someone else's freedom or character growth. The response instead asks what would be required for God to defeat the horror within the sufferer's own ultimate good.
