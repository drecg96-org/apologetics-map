# Agent summary

Anderson and Welty develop a focused metaphysical argument from logical laws. They argue that if logical laws have the necessity and normative role ordinarily attributed to them, they are best understood as necessarily existent thoughts, specifically divine thoughts.

## How to use this source

Use as a narrower and more analytically explicit descendant of the broad claim that rationality depends on God. Keep its conclusion distinct from the further claim that Christianity rather than another form of theism has been established.
