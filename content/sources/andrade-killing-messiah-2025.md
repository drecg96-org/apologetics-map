# Source profile

Andrade studies Jesus' trial and execution in Roman historical and legal context. The work distinguishes a small set of highly secure points surrounding Jesus' death from much more uncertain details in the Gospel trial narratives.

## Use

Use this source to support a careful historical claim that Jesus was executed under Pilate without importing every narrative detail into the same confidence category.

## Limit

Agreement on crucifixion does not validate later resurrection claims or settle why Pilate condemned Jesus.
