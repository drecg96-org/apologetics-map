# Agent summary

This article is useful for representing a mainstream Jewish perspective in its own terms. It explains that Judaism does not accept Jesus as Messiah, divine Son, or resurrected Lord and emphasizes that Jewish messianic expectation includes concrete redemption and transformation of the world.

## How to use this source

Use it to formulate the Jewish objection before introducing a Christian answer. It should not be treated as a complete account of the diversity of Second Temple Jewish messianic expectations or as a substitute for primary Jewish texts and specialist scholarship.
