# Agent summary

Finlay argues that many apparently categorical normative statements can be analyzed through relations to ends or standards, with context doing substantial work. This offers a reductionist alternative to reading every ordinary 'ought' as commitment to irreducible categorical normativity.

## How to use this source

Use in the ought/should branch and as a sophisticated reply to arguments that infer robust normativity directly from normative vocabulary.
