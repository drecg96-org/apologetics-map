# Source

This source gives a precise philosophical name to the strongest version of the sincere-nonbeliever case: **nonresistant nonbelief**.

A nonresistant nonbeliever does not believe that God exists but is not intentionally refusing or resisting relationship with God. That matters directly to any defense of hell that says the damned simply chose separation from God.
