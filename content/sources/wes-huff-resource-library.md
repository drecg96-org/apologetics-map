# Source profile

Wes Huff's official site collects videos, articles, recommended resources, manuscript material, and teaching on the historical reliability of the Bible. His public work emphasizes ancient manuscripts and the history and transmission of Christian texts.

## Best used for

New Testament manuscript transmission, textual variants, canon questions, Council of Nicaea misconceptions, early Christianity, Gospel reliability, and historical discussion of the resurrection.
