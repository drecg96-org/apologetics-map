# Source profile

In the course of describing Nero's punishment of Christians after the fire of Rome, Tacitus identifies the movement's namesake and reports that Christus received the extreme penalty under Pontius Pilate during Tiberius' reign.

## Use

This is a non-Christian Roman witness written decades after Jesus' death. It is useful as external corroboration that the movement was associated with an executed founder under Pilate.

## Limit

Tacitus is not an eyewitness to Jesus' execution, and this passage by itself does not establish resurrection, burial, empty tomb, or the reliability of any Gospel narrative.
