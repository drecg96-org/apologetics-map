# Source profile

Hume is a foundational skeptical source on miracle testimony.

His argument is often oversimplified as "miracles are impossible." The more useful debate point is epistemic: a reported miracle is an extraordinary event, so testimony for it must be weighed against the background evidence supporting the ordinary course of nature and against alternative explanations for the report.

Contemporary Bayesian treatments dispute parts of Hume's formulation, so use this source as a classic argument rather than the final word on miracle epistemology.
