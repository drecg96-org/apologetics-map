# Source profile

A methodological source for separating the Qur'an itself from the richer biographical material in later sira literature.

It is especially useful when evaluating claims about Muhammad's character, miracles, predictions, and life events: the relevant evidence may come from sources with different dates and historical profiles, so each claim should be assessed at the level of its actual source.
