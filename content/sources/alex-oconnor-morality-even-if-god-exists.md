# Agent summary

O'Connor argues that a divine command or divine preference does not by itself establish the truth of an ought-claim. His broader position is non-cognitivist: moral prescriptions function differently from ordinary descriptive propositions.

## How to use this source

Use it as a direct skeptical challenge to the move from God to objective normativity and as a bridge into the Euthyphro dilemma.

## Limitation

This is an older presentation. O'Connor's 2025 discussion of ethical emotivism is the better source for his current self-description, while this video remains useful for the argument directed specifically at theistic grounding.
