# Agent summary

Olson argues that ordinary moral thought makes commitments that cannot be vindicated and defends error theory against major objections. He also distinguishes what follows semantically and metaphysically from practical proposals for revising moral discourse.

## How to use this source

Use as a contemporary canonical defense of error theory alongside Mackie rather than treating error theory as only a historical position.
