# Source profile

This review synthesizes research on sensory and quasi-sensory experiences of deceased persons after bereavement.

## Relevance to resurrection debate

It establishes a general psychological background fact: experiences interpreted as encounters with a deceased person can occur in otherwise nonpsychotic bereaved people.

It does **not** establish that the earliest Christian appearance claims were generated this way. Applying modern bereavement research to particular ancient reports requires additional historical argument.
