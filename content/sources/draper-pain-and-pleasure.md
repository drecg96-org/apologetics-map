# Source profile

Draper sharpens the evidential problem of evil by framing it comparatively.

The question is not simply whether theism can be made compatible with suffering. It is whether the pattern and distribution of pain and pleasure are more expected on theism than on a relevant non-theistic alternative.

This source is useful whenever a Christian response establishes mere logical possibility but has not yet addressed comparative explanatory probability.
