# Source profile

Oppy is especially useful for steelmanning the skeptical response to natural theology.

The central pressure is not merely "I do not like these arguments." It is that, in a long-running dispute among informed and reasonable people, an argument has not succeeded dialectically if its crucial premises or standards of comparison remain reasonably rejectable by the other side.

Use this source when evaluating whether cosmological, ontological, teleological, moral, or other arguments actually move a neutral or skeptical interlocutor rather than only confirming someone who already shares key assumptions.
