# Source

Question 150 directly asks whether all transgressions of God's law are equally heinous and answers **no**: some sins are more heinous in themselves or because of aggravating circumstances.

This is especially useful for correcting the popular but imprecise slogan that "all sins are equal in God's eyes." The catechism simultaneously preserves universal human sinfulness and differing degrees of moral gravity.
