# Source profile

A foundational skeptical source for the logical problem of evil.

Of particular relevance here is Mackie's pressure on the appeal to freedom: if God is omnipotent, why could God not create persons who are genuinely free yet always freely choose the good?

Plantinga's later free-will defense is often read as a direct response to this style of argument. Keeping both sources in the graph makes the dispute visible rather than presenting "free will" as an uncontested solution.
