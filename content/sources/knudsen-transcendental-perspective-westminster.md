# Agent summary

Knudsen describes Van Til's project as asking for the conditions underlying being and meaning. On his reading, the intended foundation is God's revelation in Jesus Christ as witnessed in Scripture and therefore a full Christian theism rather than a generic deity.

He also rejects the inference that transcendental method must suppress ordinary evidence. Evidence can still be presented; the transcendental claim concerns what makes facts, meaning, and evidential reasoning possible and intelligible in the first place.

## How to use this source

Use both to state the intended strength of the Van Tilian conclusion and to support a cumulative route in which transcendental critique and historical evidence are not treated as competitors.
