# Agent summary

Kirwin analyzes disagreement from the participant's perspective and argues that important asymmetries in how agents understand their own and others' moral judgments are difficult to capture without realist structure.

## How to use this source

Use as a recent case that disagreement can itself create explanatory pressure on anti-realism.
