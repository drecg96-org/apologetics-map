# Agent summary

Frame and Hays respond to Johnson's claim that Van Tilian reasoning can at most prove generic theism and could be duplicated by a Muslim. Their reply emphasizes the concrete content of rival religious systems and argues that a purported Islamic parallel must be tested rather than assumed.

The article also moves beyond abstract method into disagreements about revelation, Scripture, the Qur'an, and the crucifixion, which makes it useful for connecting TAG to the broader Christianity-Islam comparison.

## How to use this source

Use as the direct defense against Johnson, while preserving the fact that whether the rejoinder succeeds is contested.
