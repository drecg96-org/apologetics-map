# Agent summary

Craig argues that a cluster of historical claims concerning Jesus' burial, empty tomb, postmortem appearances, and the rise of resurrection belief is best explained by bodily resurrection. Crossley challenges the inference and aspects of the underlying historical case.

## How to use this source

Use this as a two-sided debate source. Separate the relatively ordinary historical claims from the further inference to a supernatural resurrection, and track which premises each participant accepts or disputes.

## Limitation

Claims such as "established facts" or what most scholars accept are themselves contestable and should be verified through current historical scholarship rather than inherited from either debater.
