# Agent summary

Street accepts that reasons ultimately depend on contingent values. On this account an agent lacking moral concerns may not be bound in the realist categorical sense, yet this does not prevent serious practical reasoning from within evaluative standpoints.

## How to use this source

Use at the terminal subjectivist/constructivist branch to represent a developed theory that knowingly accepts contingency rather than stumbling into it.
