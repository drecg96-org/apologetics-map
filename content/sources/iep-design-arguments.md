# Source profile

A neutral survey of design arguments with a substantial fine-tuning section.

Use it for likelihood-style formulations: whether life-permitting conditions are more expected under purposive design than under specified non-design alternatives. It also helps prevent the false dichotomy that the only possibilities are God or one-universe blind chance.
