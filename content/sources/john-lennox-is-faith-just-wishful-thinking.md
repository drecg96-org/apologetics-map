# Agent summary

Lennox rejects the definition of faith as believing without evidence. He frames faith as trust that can be rationally grounded and argues that Christian belief should be open to historical and evidential examination.

## Useful debate move

When a discussion stalls on "faith versus evidence," first clarify what each person means by faith. This source is useful for distinguishing blind credulity from trust based on testimony, experience, and inference.

## Limitation

The source primarily defends the rational permissibility of faith; it does not by itself establish that Christianity's specific historical claims are true.
