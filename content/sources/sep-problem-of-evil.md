# Source profile

A broad academic reference for distinguishing different arguments from evil and different kinds of theistic reply.

It is especially useful for preventing a common collapse between:

- the logical claim that God and evil are incompatible,
- the evidential claim that some evils count against theism,
- and probabilistic arguments comparing how expected the observed world is under competing hypotheses.

Use it alongside primary skeptical sources such as Rowe and Draper and alongside primary theistic defenses.
