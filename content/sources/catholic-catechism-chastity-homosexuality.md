# Source

Sections 2357–2359 are useful here for a narrow distinction rather than as a complete argument for Christian sexual ethics.

The Catechism maintains a traditional prohibition of same-sex sexual acts while also requiring that persons with homosexual tendencies be treated with respect, compassion, and sensitivity and that unjust discrimination be avoided.

That combination illustrates why **moral evaluation** and **civil or social punishment** should not automatically be treated as the same question.
