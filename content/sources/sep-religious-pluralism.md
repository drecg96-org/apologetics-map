# Source profile

Religious diversity does not by itself prove that every religion is false or equally credible.

Its epistemic force comes from the existence of apparently sincere, informed, and reflective people who reach incompatible religious conclusions. That can create pressure for greater humility, conciliation, or an explanation of why one tradition's evidence should rationally outweigh the rest.

This is the right starting source for representing that pressure without turning pluralism into the simplistic claim that disagreement automatically makes truth impossible.
