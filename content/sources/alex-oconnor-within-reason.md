# Source profile

Alex O'Connor is a long-form interviewer, writer, and public debater focused heavily on philosophy, religion, the Bible, and ethics. He has publicly described himself as an atheist and is commonly more precisely described as an agnostic atheist.

## Best used for

Strong skeptical formulations of the problem of evil, divine hiddenness, biblical and moral objections, critique of natural-theology arguments, and careful interviews with Christian philosophers, theologians, and biblical scholars.

## Source discipline

Treat O'Connor as a skeptical/atheist perspective rather than a neutral authority on Christianity. His work is especially useful for steelmanning objections and finding where Christian arguments face pressure. When he hosts a specialist, distinguish the guest's claims from O'Connor's own position.
