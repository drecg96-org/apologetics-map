# Source

Paragraphs 1021–1022 give a clear Catholic answer to the postmortem-repentance question.

The Catechism says earthly life is the period open to accepting or rejecting divine grace and describes a particular judgment at death. It therefore does not present death as the beginning of a new ordinary opportunity to reverse one's fundamental response to grace.

This source should not be generalized into "all Christians agree." It represents official Catholic teaching and is useful alongside Protestant confessional sources and philosophical treatments of universalism or postmortem freedom.
