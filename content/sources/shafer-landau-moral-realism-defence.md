# Agent summary

Shafer-Landau develops a systematic realist position on which moral facts are objective and irreducibly normative without requiring a theistic ground. The book directly engages leading anti-realist competitors and objections to moral knowledge.

## How to use this source

Use as a central secular-realist defense and a counterweight to both anti-realism and arguments that identify objective morality with theism.
