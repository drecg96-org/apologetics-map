# Source profile

Reasonable Faith is the primary archive for William Lane Craig's apologetic and philosophical work. Its stated mission is explicitly Christian and includes material on God's existence, meaning, objective truth, moral foundations, creation, Gospel reliability, Jesus, resurrection, and religious pluralism.

## Best used for

The kalam cosmological argument, Leibnizian cosmological arguments, fine-tuning, the moral argument, divine command theory, resurrection arguments, divine foreknowledge, Molinism, atonement, and philosophy of religion.

## Source discipline

Craig is a Christian philosopher and advocate. His work is useful for rigorous formulations and replies, but claims about scholarly consensus or historical facts should be checked against independent scholarship and substantive critics.
