# Source profile

Hurtado's historical case centers on devotional practices as well as titles: prayer and invocation involving Jesus, ritual use of Jesus' name, hymnic material, and worship patterns.

Use it to establish the earliness of elevated Jesus devotion, not to skip the further philosophical and theological step from early devotion to the mature Nicene doctrine of the Trinity.
