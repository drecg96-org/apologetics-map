# Agent summary

Prinz grounds moral judgment in emotional dispositions that are substantially shaped by culture. The account offers a sophisticated alternative to both robust realism and crude individual preference theories.

## How to use this source

Use when representing relativism as an explanatory theory of moral psychology rather than as mere personal taste.
