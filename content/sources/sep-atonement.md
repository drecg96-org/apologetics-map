# Source profile

A useful neutral survey because it distinguishes **forgiveness**, **reconciliation**, **reparation**, **satisfaction**, **sacrifice**, and **punishment** rather than treating them as synonyms.

It also shows that Christianity has never had only one philosophical model of the cross. Satisfaction, penal substitution, Christus Victor, moral-influence, healing/participation, and hybrid theories try to explain different biblical motifs and face different objections.

For this graph, the source is especially important when someone asks why an omnipotent God could not simply forgive. That question partly turns on whether forgiveness alone is identical with full reconciliation and on what, if anything, the cross is claimed to accomplish beyond cancellation of guilt.
