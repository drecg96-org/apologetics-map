# Agent summary

Metaethical constructivism aims to explain normative truth without treating it as fixed by stance-independent normative facts. Different versions appeal to idealized rational agreement, constitutive standards of agency, or other procedures internal to practical reasoning.

## How to use this source

Use as the strongest sophisticated alternative to simple actual-attitude subjectivism. It shows why moving from present preferences to idealized rational standards is a substantive theory rather than an automatic concession to robust moral realism.
