# Agent summary

Enoch asks exactly how disagreement is supposed to undermine moral realism and separates distinct explanatory and epistemic arguments that are often run together. His central conclusion is that disagreement alone has little force against realism unless supplemented by further premises about explanation, reliability, or rational response.

## How to use this source

Use as a realist reply to the disagreement objection and to force the inference from disagreement to anti-realism to be stated explicitly.
