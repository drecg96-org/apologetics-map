# Agent summary

Craig and Harris agree for purposes of the debate that morality is not merely personal preference, but disagree over its foundation. Craig argues that natural facts cannot by themselves generate binding moral duties and proposes God's nature and commands as the foundation. Harris connects moral evaluation to facts about the well-being of conscious creatures.

## Why it matters for the map

The exchange isolates an important branch that simple relativism misses: a secular thinker may affirm objective moral evaluation while attempting to naturalize it. That makes the is/ought and grounding questions explicit.
