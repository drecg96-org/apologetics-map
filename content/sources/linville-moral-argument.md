# Agent summary

Linville treats several moral phenomena as evidence relevant to worldview comparison, including objective value, epistemic access, and human dignity. He also engages the effect of evolutionary naturalism on moral knowledge.

## How to use this source

Use in the main moral argument and as a bridge between grounding and evolutionary epistemology.
