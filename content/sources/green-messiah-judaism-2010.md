Use this source to prevent the comparison from projecting one later rabbinic formulation backward as though every first-century Jew shared an identical messianic checklist.

Diversity does not by itself prove Jesus was Messiah. It changes the historical question from "Did Jesus match the one Jewish expectation?" to "Which expectations were present, and how well does the Christian claim fit them?"
