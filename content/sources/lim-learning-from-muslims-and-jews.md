# Agent summary

Lim examines historical Muslim-Christian and Jewish-Christian encounters rather than treating interreligious disagreement as a modern invention. The article highlights disputes over the divine identity of Christ, the Trinity, and rival claims that a newer revelation fulfills or corrects an older one.

## How to use this source

Use it to frame recurring comparative questions and to avoid presenting Christianity-Islam or Christianity-Judaism as a collection of isolated proof texts. It is especially useful for the structure of competing fulfillment claims.
