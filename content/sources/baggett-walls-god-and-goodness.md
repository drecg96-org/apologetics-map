# Agent summary

The chapter develops the claim that a necessarily good divine nature can ground value while commands grounded in that nature can play a role in obligation. This is intended to avoid both arbitrary voluntarism and an external independent standard.

## How to use this source

Use in the divine-nature response to Euthyphro and in the grounding comparison.
