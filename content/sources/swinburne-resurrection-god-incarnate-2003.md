# Source profile

Swinburne makes background probability explicit. On his approach, evidence for theism and reasons God might have for incarnation affect how surprising a resurrection would be before the direct historical data are considered.

## How to use this source

Use it to represent a serious Bayesian alternative to both:

- excluding miracles by methodological rule; and
- treating testimony as sufficient without considering prior probability.

The numerical and theological assumptions remain highly contestable.
