# Source profile

A classic modern defense against the **logical** problem of evil.

Plantinga's goal is limited but important: show that an omnipotent, perfectly good God and moral evil need not be logically inconsistent. The defense relies on a libertarian conception of morally significant freedom and the possibility that not every world containing such freedom is feasible for God to actualize while also guaranteeing that creatures always choose rightly.

This does not by itself explain why God permits the actual amount or distribution of suffering. The evidential problem of evil remains a separate question.
