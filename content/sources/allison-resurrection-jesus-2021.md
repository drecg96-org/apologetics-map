# Source profile

Allison offers a deliberately non-binary treatment of the resurrection question. The book examines early formulas and confessions, reported appearances, burial and empty-tomb traditions, parallels to postmortem experiences, competing explanatory stories, and both apologetic and skeptical overreach.

## How to use this source

Use Allison especially when the map needs to distinguish:

- a historical datum from the explanation placed on it;
- what can be argued from early Christian traditions;
- visionary or bereavement-experience parallels;
- the empty-tomb debate;
- and the point where historical reconstruction gives way to broader worldview or metaphysical judgment.

This is useful precisely because the work does not function as a simple apologetic proof text.
