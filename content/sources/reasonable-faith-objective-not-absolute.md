# Agent summary

Craig distinguishes three concepts that are often conflated. Objective moral truth is independent of human opinion; an absolute rule applies regardless of circumstances; a universal rule applies to everyone. A moral judgment can therefore be objective while still being sensitive to relevant circumstances.

## How to use this source

This is a useful terminology source near the beginning of the objective-morality branch. The distinction is not uniquely theistic and should be treated as conceptual clarification rather than evidence for God.
