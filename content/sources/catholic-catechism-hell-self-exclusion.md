# Source

Paragraph 1033 is especially relevant to the free-choice framing. It defines hell as definitive self-exclusion from communion with God and the blessed.

The surrounding paragraphs are equally important because the Catechism does not reduce hell to a purely self-generated psychological state. It also speaks of judgment, condemnation, and punishment. That makes it a useful source for a both/and model rather than a simplistic choice-versus-punishment dichotomy.
