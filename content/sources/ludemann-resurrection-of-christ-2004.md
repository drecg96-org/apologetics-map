# Source profile

Lüdemann offers a developed skeptical reconstruction of the resurrection traditions. He treats the reported appearances as historically significant while denying that they establish a bodily resurrection, instead locating the origin of Easter faith in visionary experience.

## How to use this source

Use it to steelman a vision-based naturalistic alternative and to keep the map from treating sincere appearance claims as equivalent to supernatural causation.

Its psychological reconstruction is itself contestable and should not be presented as settled scholarship.
