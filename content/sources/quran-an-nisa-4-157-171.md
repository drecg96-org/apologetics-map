# Agent summary

Verses 4:157-158 deny that Jesus was killed or crucified and describe God as raising him. Verse 4:171 calls Jesus the Messiah, a messenger of God, God's word conveyed to Mary, and a spirit from God, while rejecting the instruction to say "Three" and affirming God's oneness.
