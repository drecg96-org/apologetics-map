# Source profile

Licona develops an explicitly historiographical defense of treating the resurrection as a historical explanatory question. He surveys historical method, identifies a set of historical data, and compares the resurrection hypothesis with rival explanations.

## How to use this source

Use Licona as a strong affirmative source for:

- inference to the best explanation;
- explicit historical criteria;
- comparison with naturalistic alternatives;
- and the claim that miracle hypotheses need not be excluded from historical reasoning in advance.

Do not use it as a neutral statement of scholarly consensus. It is an argued defense and should be paired with critical and mixed scholarship.
