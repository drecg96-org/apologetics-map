# Source profile

The entry surveys what contemporary metaphysical Platonism claims, why philosophers adopt it, and the principal objections.

Of particular relevance here, it develops the epistemological argument against Platonism: if abstract objects are nonspatiotemporal and causally isolated, a theory still owes an account of how human thinkers can have reliable knowledge concerning them.

## How to use this source

This source helps keep the worldview comparison precise. Platonism offers a serious non-theistic route to objective abstract truth, but that route has a well-developed epistemological burden.

## Limitation

The entry surveys an active dispute rather than announcing a consensus refutation of Platonism.
