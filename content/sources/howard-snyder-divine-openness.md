# Source

Howard-Snyder targets a key premise in Schellenberg's argument: that unsurpassable divine love must entail openness to conscious reciprocal relationship with every capable person at every moment.

He argues that this entailment should not simply be assumed.

That does not prove why any particular case of hiddenness occurs. It instead narrows the dispute to a more precise question about what perfect love requires and whether delayed or indirect forms of relationship can ever be compatible with it.
