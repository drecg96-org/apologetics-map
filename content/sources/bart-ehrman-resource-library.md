# Source profile

Bart Ehrman is a New Testament and early-Christianity scholar whose public work is useful for historical-critical challenges to popular apologetic claims about the Bible, Jesus, and the resurrection.

## Best used for

- textual criticism and manuscript transmission,
- authorship and dating questions,
- the historical Jesus,
- diversity in early Christianity,
- historical methodology surrounding resurrection and miracle claims.

## Source discipline

Do not treat a creator hub as a citation for every position associated with its author. Prefer the specific book, paper, lecture, debate, or episode that actually makes the claim.

Also distinguish historical scholarship from broader worldview conclusions. A historical-critical conclusion about a text does not by itself establish atheism, just as a successful apologetic argument about one text would not by itself establish Christianity.
