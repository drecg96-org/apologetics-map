# Agent summary

Lennox connects objective moral value to a transcendent account of human beings made in God's image. The argument is especially relevant to equal human dignity and the question of why humans possess value that is not dependent on intelligence, power, usefulness, or social approval.

## Limitation

The source is an apologetic interview, not a detailed metaethical defense. It is best used to identify the human-worth branch and then paired with philosophical work on moral realism and human dignity.
