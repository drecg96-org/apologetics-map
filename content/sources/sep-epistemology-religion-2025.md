# Source profile

A neutral entry for evaluating whether religious experience and testimony can count as evidence without treating them as self-authenticating.

It is useful for a cumulative case because religious experience can contribute defeasible evidence while remaining vulnerable to disagreement, alternative explanations, and tradition-specific interpretation.
