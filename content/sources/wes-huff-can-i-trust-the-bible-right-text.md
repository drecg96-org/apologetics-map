# Agent summary

This entry is useful for the common objection that centuries of copying necessarily make the New Testament text unknowable. Huff explains the manuscript-comparison and textual-critical framework used to reconstruct earlier readings.

## How to use this source

Use it when the debate moves from "the Bible has variants" to the more precise question of whether variants prevent us from knowing the text. Keep textual reliability distinct from the separate questions of historical truth, inspiration, and interpretation.

## Limitation

This is an apologetic overview. For disputed textual variants or quantitative claims, consult critical editions and specialist textual scholarship.
