# Source profile

A direct philosophical critique of Plantinga's evolutionary argument against naturalism.

Use it to distinguish the premise about conditional reliability from the further defeater step. Even granting uncertainty about P(reliable cognition | naturalism and evolution) does not automatically show that a naturalist must distrust every cognitive deliverance.
