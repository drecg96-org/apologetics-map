# Agent summary

Joyce combines an evolutionary account of the human moral sense with metaethical skepticism. The important distinction is between explaining why moral capacities evolved and determining whether the resulting moral beliefs are epistemically warranted.

## How to use this source

Use in the evolutionary-debunking and error-theory branches as a major book-length skeptical treatment.
