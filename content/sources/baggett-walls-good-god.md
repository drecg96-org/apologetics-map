# Agent summary

Baggett and Walls argue that classical theism offers an integrated explanation of moral facts, duties, human worth, freedom, and responsibility. Their approach is cumulative and comparative rather than depending on one simple divine-command inference.

## How to use this source

Use as a major contemporary theistic grounding defense while keeping secular competitors in view.
