# Source

Sections 1854–1864 state that sins are evaluated according to their gravity and distinguish mortal from venial sin. The treatment also emphasizes knowledge and consent when assessing culpability.

This provides a major non-Protestant example of the same broad point found in the Westminster tradition: universal sinfulness does not mean that every sinful act is identical in gravity.
