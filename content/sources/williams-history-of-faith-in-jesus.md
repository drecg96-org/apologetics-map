# Agent summary

Williams argues that devotion to Jesus as a living agent appears within the earliest Christian decades, including invocation of Jesus and direct address to him in Christian worship. This is relevant to the question of whether high Christology is simply a much later invention.

## How to use this source

Use it as evidence for the early-devotion branch, not as a complete proof of Jesus' divinity. A later pass should add scholarship that offers competing explanations for the same early data.
