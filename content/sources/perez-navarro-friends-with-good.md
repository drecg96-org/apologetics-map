# Agent summary

The paper directly challenges a common anti-relativist argument from moral progress. It develops an assessment-sensitive form of relativism on which past claims can be judged false from a present standpoint while future assessors may in turn revise present judgments.

## How to use this source

Use to prevent the map from claiming that relativism cannot speak coherently about progress. The sharper issue is whether progress is only standpoint-relative or whether some reforms are better independently of every standpoint.
