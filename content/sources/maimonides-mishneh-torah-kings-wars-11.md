# Agent summary

Maimonides' Laws of Kings and Wars 11 gives a highly influential Jewish formulation of messianic expectations. The Messiah restores the Davidic kingdom, rebuilds the Temple, gathers Israel's dispersed, strengthens Torah observance, and ultimately leads the nations toward worship of God. The chapter explicitly says that a candidate who fails or is killed is not the promised Messiah and applies that judgment to Jesus.

## How to use this source

Use it as a primary Jewish source for the later rabbinic argument against Jesus' messiahship. Do not project Maimonides' medieval formulation wholesale back into every strand of first-century Jewish expectation; pair it with Second Temple scholarship when the historical question is being asked.
