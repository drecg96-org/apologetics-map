# Agent summary

Among respondents to the main 2020 PhilPapers survey, 62.07% accepted or leaned toward moral realism and 26.12% accepted or leaned toward moral anti-realism, with the remainder selecting other responses. On the separate moral-judgment question, 69.25% accepted or leaned toward cognitivism and 20.72% toward non-cognitivism.

## How to use this source

This is sociological evidence about professional philosophical opinion, not evidence that the majority position is true. Its value is chiefly corrective: moral realism is a live mainstream philosophical position and should not be presented as uniquely religious or philosophically obsolete.
