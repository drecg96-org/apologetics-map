# Source

Chapter 32 presents a classic Reformed account in which death fixes a person's intermediate state: the righteous are received into heaven and the wicked are in hell awaiting the resurrection and final judgment.

The chapter does not describe a postmortem evangelistic opportunity or a later reversal from one state to the other.

Use this as a specific Reformed confessional witness, not as a substitute for surveying all Christian traditions.
