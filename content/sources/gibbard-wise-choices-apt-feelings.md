# Agent summary

Gibbard analyzes normative judgment through attitudes about what is rational or appropriate. The view aims to preserve disagreement, reasoning, and practical guidance without treating moral utterances as straightforward reports of stance-independent properties.

## How to use this source

Use as a canonical sophisticated non-cognitivist alternative, especially when simple emotivism is too crude.
