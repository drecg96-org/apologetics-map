# Source profile

A useful survey for separating several claims that are often compressed into the word **sin**: individual wrongful acts, corrupt dispositions, a fallen condition, alienation from God, and moral evil.

The entry also situates free-will defenses within the broader problem of evil. This is useful because explaining the possibility of a first wrongful choice is not automatically the same as explaining inherited consequences, natural evil, or the full distribution of suffering.
