# Agent summary

Cowie challenges the move from epistemic normativity to moral normativity. The paper is useful because it targets the analogy itself rather than merely denying epistemic norms.

## How to use this source

Use as a serious counterbranch after the epistemic companions-in-guilt argument: the realist must establish a relevant similarity between moral and epistemic normativity rather than assume it.
