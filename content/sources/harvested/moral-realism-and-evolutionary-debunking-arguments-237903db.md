# Agent summary

Shafer-Landau's chapter addresses evolutionary debunking arguments from the standpoint of moral realism. Cambridge places the chapter in the handbook's section titled "Against Debunking Arguments," making it useful as a realist counterweight to sources that infer broad moral skepticism from the evolutionary origins of human evaluative tendencies.

The key dialectical distinction for the map is between explaining the causal genealogy of a moral belief and showing that the belief lacks justification. Evolutionary influence becomes debunking only if it supplies a reason to distrust the truth-sensitivity or reliability of the relevant moral-belief process.
