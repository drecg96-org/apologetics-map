# Agent summary

Hare develops a moral argument that emphasizes the relationship between moral demand, human limitation, practical hope, and divine assistance. It is broader than a simple argument from objective facts to a commander.

Use as a distinct theistic moral-argument strand rather than conflating all moral arguments with divine-command theory.
