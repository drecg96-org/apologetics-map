# Source profile

This passage is central to the later-revelation comparison because it simultaneously:

- describes the Torah as containing guidance and light;
- describes the Gospel given to Jesus as guidance and light;
- tells the People of the Gospel to judge by what God revealed therein;
- presents the Qur'an as confirming previous scripture and functioning as a criterion over it.

That combination makes the precise meaning of later Muslim claims about corruption or supersession an important interpretive question.
