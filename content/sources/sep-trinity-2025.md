# Source profile

A current philosophical map of classical and contemporary Trinity models.

Use it to keep two questions distinct:

- whether a trinitarian doctrine can be stated coherently while preserving monotheism;
- whether the historical and scriptural evidence gives positive reason to accept such a doctrine.

The entry also records substantial disagreement about how directly the later creedal doctrine is present in the earliest Christian sources.
