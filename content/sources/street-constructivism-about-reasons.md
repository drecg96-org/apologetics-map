# Agent summary

Street argues that reasons can be constructed from an agent's evaluative standpoint rather than discovered as independent normative entities. The view can generate criticism and coherence requirements while remaining fundamentally standpoint-dependent.

## How to use this source

Use as a sophisticated answer to desire-independent-reasons arguments and as an alternative to both realism and simple actual-desire subjectivism.
