# Source profile

This classic study helped popularize cognitive dissonance as an explanation for why a deeply committed group might reinterpret failure rather than immediately abandon a belief.

## Limitation for resurrection debate

The analogy to Jesus' followers is indirect. The social setting, evidence, expectations, and historical documentation differ greatly, so cognitive dissonance should be treated as a possible mechanism rather than a ready-made explanation of Easter faith.
